Okay so I'm going to give a little bit of background information before diving in just so there's a bit of context. In high school, I "George," was close friends with three guys. "Tim," "Matt," and the friend of concern here, "Kevin." We were all tight in high school and helped each other along in a bunch of ways, both personal and not.

Now, round about sophomore year of college, things start taking a turn. Basically, none of us went to the same school so we would use Skype to talk while playing video games or doing whatever. During one of these Skype calls, Kevin shows up drunk and starts slinging insults. In his mind he was probably just using the same humour that we usually used with each other, but to the sober ones he was opening up with both barrels.

That being said, this behavior increases and he gets increasingly belligerent with us, especially when we don't agree with him. At some point, Tim decides enough is enough and cuts off contact with him. Matt and I continue talking to him, mostly because we can see that underneath the layers he's a good guy who just needs direction.

Eventually though it gets to be too much for me as well and I leave our group chat too. Time passes. I'm not talking to him, but Matt keeps contact due to an incredible amount of patience and because he's just geographically closer.

After some months, I get a couple apologetic texts from Kevin asking for advice and I give it to him. Matt tells me that Kevin seems like he's actually changing for the better so I decide to go ahead and make a new group chat between the three of us.

The thing is, this guy was once one of my closest friends. One of my only friends back then, quite honestly. If I hadn't been helping him figure his life out through high school and early college, I would've made like Tim and just stopped caring a long time ago.
