As much as 60% of today’s crude oil price is pure speculation driven by large trader banks and hedge funds.

It has nothing to do with the convenient myths of Peak Oil.

The oil bubble is in fact being fueled by speculation.

The biggest reason for the rise of oil prices in America has to do with the devaluation of the dollar.

Oil's rise against the Euro has been accurate and a reflection of supply and demand.

This does worry me a bit that these major investment firms who are so into this speculation will see nasty fallout from the eventual oil bubble crash.

This is not the same as an actual financial bubble, which is a result of people dogpiling an investment to an extent that does not reflect its value hoping to be the last to get out, very much like a pyramid scheme.

Speculation is rational behavior under the right circumstances.

The bubble occurs when people buy simply because they expect other people to buy.

The reality is that no one really knows what the price will be come say...September.

The fact that there is any question that a non-renewable resource will run out just shows how poorly the educational system is serving people.
