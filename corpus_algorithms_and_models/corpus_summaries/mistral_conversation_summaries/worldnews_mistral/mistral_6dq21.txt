The global perception that all Americans are flag-draping jingoists is a pretty tired cliche at this point.

I think it's funny that a lot of us seem to think that we have some sort of lead or exclusivity to our "freedom".

I love the principles America was founded on.

I don't love what it has become or what it is doing.

I stay because I believe we can bring back those founding principles.

I think the only thing we're currently #1 in is in military might.

I love the IDEAL of the US.

It is a constant work in progress.

I love America, simply for the reason that I'd rather be living here, than anywhere else in the world.

I love America because it is far from perfect.

It is a nation of underdogs, individualists, romantics and cynics.

It is a nation filled with people who strive to make it the best it can be.

It is also a nation the epitomizes the meaning of "freedom".

We welcome everyone, even those who hate us.

There are just as many of us who are humble in our admission that we don't act the way we always should, as those who shrilly proclaim that America beats all.

To answer the original question, I think most Americans are expected to "love" America, simply because they live in the greatest experimental playground for freedom and democracy that the world has ever known.

But they're also expected to love their fellow Americans, regardless of race, creed or political belief.

Oddly enough, America seems to behave the worst, when it stops behaving like America should.

If we're flawed at all, then it's because we sometimes forget who and what we should be...and what we COULD be.

A GOOD example for the rest of the world.
