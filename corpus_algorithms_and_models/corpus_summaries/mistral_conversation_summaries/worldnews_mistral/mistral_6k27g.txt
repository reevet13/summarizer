Personally, I think it's entirely irrelevant whether Scientology is called a 'cult' or a 'religion'.

There's no real distinction between them, except perhaps that religions are more successful at extracting more money from more people.

Let's not follow Britain down this route.

Free speech and freedom of thought also includes freedom to criticize or even hate any group you wish.

"Nothing in this Part shall be read or given effect in a way which prohibits or restricts discussion, criticism or expressions of antipathy, dislike, ridicule, insult or abuse of particular religions or the beliefs or practices of their adherents."

The fact that something is enshrined in law does not make it beyond reproach or criticism.

So it's arguable if it breaches the law at all - this is clearly a discretionary matter - the police can decide whether to prosecute or not.
