Taiwan has a Buddhist nun called [Cheng Yen who's known as the Mother Teresa of Taiwan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheng_Yen).

She's done tons of charity work, opened up free clinics, hospitals, nursing colleges and provides aid to the poor around the world.

It's a pity that she doesn't get the recognition that the Dalai Lama does.

Anyway, it's good to see China try a more peaceful resolution to this.

When I talk to Taiwanese, one of their main concerns about reunification is this: would Taiwan be treated as autonomous like Hong Kong is, or would the island be treated as "autonomous" like Tibet is?

If China granted Tibet a bit more political autonomy and allowed the Dalai Lama to return, it would mitigate the Tibetan resistance while at the same time showing Taiwan that they would have little to fear being absorbed into the Mainland.

The KMT victory in March suggests that Taiwanese are looking to at least improve relations with the mainland which hopefully means that this conflict is ultimately resolvable.

I think it's great that they'll working things out diplomatically.
