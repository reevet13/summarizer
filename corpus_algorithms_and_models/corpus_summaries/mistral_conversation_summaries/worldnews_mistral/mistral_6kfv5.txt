There are many large, stable systems both artificial and natural which do not need a centralized mechanism to keep the systems "healthy".

humans are generally pretty good about making decisions about subjects that they have a lot of direct experience with.

a "free market" situation (where all decisions are completely between seller & buyer) can be a VERY efficient way of allocating resources (assuming that both seller & buyer have good information & there is a reasonable amount of competition in the market).

you can have large, stable & adaptable systems which do not have a central decision-making mechanism.
