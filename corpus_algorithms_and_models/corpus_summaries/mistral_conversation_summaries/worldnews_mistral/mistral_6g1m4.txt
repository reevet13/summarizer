Forensics is a constantly-evolving field.

The trick to having a fair justice system is to have a system that's willing to go back and say, "Hey, I think we made a mistake," when new technologies allow it to re-examine an old case.

Many studies have been conducted over the years and the veracity of eye-witness testimony in the court room has been proven numerous times to be extremely unreliable.
