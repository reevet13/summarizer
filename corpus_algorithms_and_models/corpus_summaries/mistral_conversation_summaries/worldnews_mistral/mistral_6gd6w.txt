ASDA made it's name achieving C *before* the Wal-Mart takeover, but they copied the Wal-Mart management style directly - there were *very* close links between Wal-Mart and ASDA for years before Wal-Mart bought ASDA out.

I remember walking into a Wal-Mart in the states years ago, and being astonished at how identical it was to my local ASDA.

Wal-Mart financial backing allowed them to expand more so, but not a huge amount more than the growth they experienced prior to the takeover.

The break-up and sale of British Leyland was disgusting - a travesty of justice that should never have happened.

Jaguar, Aston-Martin, Land-Rover, Rolls-Royce and Bentley all suffered from the same problem - a lack of capital compared with foreign (government backed) rivals, leading to an inability to create new market leading (and therefore saleable) models.

The problem was we backed up our industry then we pulled the plug on them.
