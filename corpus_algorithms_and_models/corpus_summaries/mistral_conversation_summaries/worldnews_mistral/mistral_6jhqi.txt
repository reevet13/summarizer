A modern house or commercial building as built in America will not collapse in a large earthquake.

Where buildings are built with poorly reinforced concrete, unreinforced concrete, and unreinforced masonry, like China, they just crumble.

Japan's buildings are just as resistant to earthquakes as American buildings are, but 6500+ perished in the Kobe earthquake (6.8).

The Richter magnitude scale is a base-10 logarithmic scale, so the China quake (magnitude 7.9 according to USGS) was more than ten times as powerful as the Kobe quake.

In a mag 7.8 quake, the shaking can go on for 2 or 3 minutes and at, or very near, the epicenter, you're going to be hard pressed to find a building that can withstand those kind of loads for that length of time.

What's truly awful about this quake is that the pictures we're seeing aren't being taken at the epicenter - the roads into that region are gone.

Say it with me: China is a **developing** country.
