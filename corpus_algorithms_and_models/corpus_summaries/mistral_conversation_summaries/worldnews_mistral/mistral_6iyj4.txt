I think the biggest problem, at least in the UK, is that you are set up to be in debt.

You go to uni and you have to take up a loan.

I left with almost £20000 worth of debt, and that's before getting a "real" job.

If you already have this amount of debt, you have absolutely no qualms about taking out a credit card or seven.

The government has an anti-education policy (a well educated society would never vote Labour) and like to make big speeches about reducing poverty and debt but then pass policies that serve to do the complete opposite.

I've been out of uni for almost three years, earn well above average, yet still have no disposable income because I'm still paying off debts the government's education policy forced me into.

I don't have any debts, and gathered savings of around 50k.

Lessons learnt: Never form any sort of debt, take care of your savings, work in the right industry, do stuff you like in your free time, never hesitate to take chances, built a strong network and watch your spending.
