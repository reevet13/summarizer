This is bullshit.

The rules of congress need to be changed to make anonymous votes illegal.

Our government is not authorized to simply take a copy of our genetic material.

An anonymous study of population genetics might be tolerable for better research purposes, but this genetic branding of every individual born in the united states crosses the line.

I could see a lot of potential abuse of this kind of information, not limited to genetic discrimination and national health care 'emergencies' where the government tries to obtain the right to dictate a group's life based on their genetic map.

There is a difference between performing a one-time screening and establishing a mandatory, nation-wide genetic database.

Newborn testing is already in place in every state.

In the end, is it more important that your kid be omitted from a database, or that your kid lives beyond a week?
