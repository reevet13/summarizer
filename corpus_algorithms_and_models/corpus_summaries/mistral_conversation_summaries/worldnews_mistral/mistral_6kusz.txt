China's one child policy sounds evil at first, but just think about it for a second.

With over a billion people in China already, what would happen if people started having as many children as they wanted?

You think India is bad? Just wait and see what would happen if China drops the one child policy.

The penalty for having a second child in China is a steep $100 fine with compulsory sterilization of the mother.

China's strict family planning laws permit urban married couples to have only one child each.

Liang Yage and his wife Wei Linrong had one child and believed that — like many other couples — they could pay a fine and keep their second baby.

Wei was 7 months pregnant when 10 family planning officials visited her at home on April 16.

The couple was then driven to Youjiang district maternity hospital in Baise city.

The family planning people said forced abortions and forced sterilizations were both being carried out.

The officials gave Wei three injections in the lower abdomen. Contractions started the next afternoon, and continued for almost 16 hours. Her child was stillborn.

China has 3.1 times the land area of India. India's population is 1.13 billion, China's is 1.32 billion. Imagine a China with 3.5 billion people.
