Food supplies should not be controlled by folks who have an economic interest in keeping us dependent upon them.

Having corn that cannot be replanted is only good for Monsanto, not good for consumers.

Creating crops resistant to intense pesticides and herbicides so that you can use more and more chemicals on the field is great for the manufacturers of pesticides and herbicides -- which is why you see them directing research that way, instead of focusing on making more resistant plants.

We end up with chemical-laden food that costs more than it needs to, because big agribusiness wants to keep farmers tethered to using their proprietary products.

It's not really much different from when mining companies used to pay miners in company scrip, which could only be used to buy things at the (overpriced) company store.

Farmers today are barely getting by while big ag profits go to the companies that are screwing them over.

The air you breathe, the water you drink, and the food you eat has all felt the impact of agribusinesses' "profit above all" strategy.

If Monsanto truly makes their money by producing an inferior product that is over-priced and fucks-over farmers, it should be easy to put them out of business.

Monsanto uses their lawyers much like the MPAA or RIAA, using them as an intimidation force.

Monsanto has consistently shown a willingness to take people to court using RIAA-like scare tactics to get farmers who won't cooperate.
