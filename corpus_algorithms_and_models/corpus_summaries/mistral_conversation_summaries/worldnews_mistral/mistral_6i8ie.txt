The Dutch have been doing this since the 15th century: beer, tobacco, drugs.

It's not that they're liberal, it's that they're practical.

I still think legalisation is better than what we do now.

Expenditures on jails and police would go down if drugs were legalized.

Drugs should be taxed sufficiently to cover the costs the drugs cause to society.

Nearly *all* of the damage to society ascribed to drugs is actually caused by their prohibition.
