Essentially, their acetaldehyde dehydrogenase is weaker than in other people, so they cannot process acetaldehyde (which the body metabolizes from alcohol, and is 30 times more toxic) as quickly.

It accumulates and makes them feel sick.

To my experience, as an asian (and thus in the "asian community"), we don't drink nearly as much - at least in quantity, if not in regularity.

Japan is #1 for the world:
>The latest survey by Japan’s Ministry of Health and Welfare (MHW) found that an estimated 67 percent of the 126,686,000 population drink, and almost 2.5 million, or about 2 percent, are alcoholics or heavy drinkers.

150ml of alcohol = 3 liters of Japanese beer (usually 5%) or 4.7 liters (more than US gallon) of American beer (3.2%).

One in 50 Japanese drinks that much (counting children).

Two in three Japanese drink, counting children.

Korea isn't far behind.

This is not a new study or finding. It has been known that many asians have lower levels of alcohol dehydrogenase activity.

Not ALL but many.
