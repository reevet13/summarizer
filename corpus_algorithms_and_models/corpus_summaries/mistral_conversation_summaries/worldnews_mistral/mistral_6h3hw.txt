ARTICLE 9. Aspiring sincerely to an international peace based on justice and order, the Japanese people forever renounce war as a sovereign right of the nation and the threat or use of force as means of settling international disputes.
In order to accomplish the aim of the preceding paragraph, land, sea, and air forces, as well as other war potential, will never be maintained.

As such, as far as I understand it, when the legislative branch of the government rules that something in unconstitutional, the other branches must either pass laws to make future actions in that vein legal, or give up that course of action.

In other words: it's unconstitutional (and illegal) for Prime Minister Fukuda Yasuo to say "I will not be affected by the court decision." He must bow to the judiciary and end the unconstitutional practise by the JSDF or he must rewrite the law that makes the JSDFs actions illegal (in this case amend the constitution.)
