Can we please move beyond this old-notion of the Olympics being all about humanity (devoid of all it's politics) coming together to celebrate the achievements of it's best.

The fact is, that the Olympics, as it exists today, is a politically relevant entity. It is not pure.

In fact, it never has been. If countries had boycotted the Nazi's in 1936, then I'm sure it would have had some bad consequences. But it still would have been morally right to do it. And in retrospect, they should have.
