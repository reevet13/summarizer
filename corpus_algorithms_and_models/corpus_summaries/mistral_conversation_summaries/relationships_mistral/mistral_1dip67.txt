My father passed away unexpectedly.

I stopped college and took a job in her town.

This bunny was her beloved child and favorite between us all.

She only wanted me to fix them in so much as they would not affect my sibling.

I slowly repaired the damage that I could.

I learned quick about this new life full of evils.

My mother loaned money she didn't have (including mine) at the slick bunny's insistence.

Professional help for my mother never went far.

I kept her finances in hand (with her permission) as a precaution.

I may never be free of keeping a wary eye on my mother at this rate.

Today, however, is the tale of my trying to save my mother again from my sibling.

I would get a guardianship if it meant I could kick the free-loader to the side and limit what my mother could do for my sibling.

I really just want her to be happy.

I don't feel I'm able to judge as I've been trapped in this for nearly three years running now.
