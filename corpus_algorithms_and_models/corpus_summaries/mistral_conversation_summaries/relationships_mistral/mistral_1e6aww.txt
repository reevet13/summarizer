She lives LITERALLY in the middle of nowhere, I’m talking one way dirt track up a hill with very few other houses around, lifestock all over the place, thick bush surroundings, mountains in the distance, etc.

Her dad runs towards us as she gets cut off mid sentence with “go to your room”.
