We met online through a friend, and hit it off quickly.

We made plans for me to move in together in an apartment when we both got out of high school.

This was a plan we'd agreed on totally, and was my plan after high school.

For the past few months, I've been experiencing a textbook case of "the fade".

Last night, he told me all the things that he's been upset about with me.

The 4 main things he mentioned was how I treat my family, that I wasn't a good source of artistic/general motivation, that we were too different in likes and dislikes to be compatible, and how easily my opinions change.

He's voiced some of these concerns before, but never with such urgency or all at once like this.

I didn't think he was this upset, although the fade was starting to worry me greatly.

I am on his side. I don't talk kindly to my family.

He helped me through that, making our relationship all the stronger, but it put a lot of hate in it towards my family that wouldn't be there otherwise.

But this is something I can change, is it not?

The second issue, lack of motivation, is a harder issue to tackle.

We seem to be in a somewhat similar position, but he doesn't acknowledge that he drops things as well.

The third issue is confusing to a degree.

I think it was both. And I think I can fix this.

This cycle has happened several times on several topics, and it seems like something we can fix instantly with a bit more understanding between the both of us.

I know that some of these issues seem large, but on a grand scale, they are all climbable.

This relationship is the best thing to ever happen to me, and the most important thing in my life right now.
