During our travel, we went out to a bar, which, after a few drinks Ron was doing his own thing elsewhere and she kissed me a few times on the cheek, not a huge deal, drunk/happy kisses.

Tom laughed and brought up that Grace was really warming up to me, and even told him that she had a huge crush on me and was debating whether she should let her boyfriend (Ron) know that she had a crush on me.

Ron leaves on an out-of-the-country trip, and long-story-short, he comes back and breaks it off with Grace.
