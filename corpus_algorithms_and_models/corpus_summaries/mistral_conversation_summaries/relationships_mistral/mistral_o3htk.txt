We met 1.5 years ago, moved in about a year ago and got engaged.

Relationship has always been... rocky.

Things calmed down a bit and for a while everything was good.

Trust has always been a big issue with her... as in her not trusting me, and as in her not being trustworthy either.

She has had some minor transgressions that I'm aware of... emails to certain guys that she lied about, phone calls that she tried to keep secret from me, etc.

I don't know for a fact that she ever cheated on me, but I think so.

Fast forward to new years eve, we'd been fighting for a couple of days.

At midnight she was nowhere near me, so I tracked her down and gave her a little kiss, which she barely acknowledged.

Huge fight starts once again, and by the next morning she had basically said she wanted to break up.

I got home and asked her one last time if there was any chance in her mind that we could fix things... she said no.

Then yesterday, I've been gone 2 days and I'm staying at a friends' house.

Then just today she's texting me, tells me she's heartbroken.

I started to explain myself a little bit, felt horrible, tried to tell her I was really just kind of lost and tumbling through space.

But then it was bugging me, so I did some digging around and found out that she had built herself a profile on there too. LAST SUMMER.

I was hurt, but I basically sent her an email and said hey, I don't hate you, I guess we both did the same thing, how upset can either one of us be at the other.

The thing is, I still love her. I really, really love her and it rips my heart out to think about breaking up with her.
