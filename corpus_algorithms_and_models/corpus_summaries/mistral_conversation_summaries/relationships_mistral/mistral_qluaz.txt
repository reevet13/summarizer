H says he feels like our relationship can’t change unless we break up, and that he’s very confused with who he is and what his identity is.

He also told me he doesn’t think we can grow up unless we are apart and that he feels like I’ll maintain my abandonment complex and he’ll constantly remain feeling inadequate unless we take separate.

Regardless of the bumps in the road, I love this guy with my entire heart, body and soul, and am willing to go through strife for him.
