He is also very guarded about his emotions, and generally has a serious personality.
I am more of an emotional, playful person who likes to form deep emotional attachments with other people.

I never got to have those strong "butterfly" feelings for my boyfriend, or experience those sweeping, passionate feelings.

I have grown to truly love and care about my boyfriend, but my feelings have always been more muted.

We have been dating for 3.5 years and still never discuss things like marriage, kids, future house/career/plans/etc.
