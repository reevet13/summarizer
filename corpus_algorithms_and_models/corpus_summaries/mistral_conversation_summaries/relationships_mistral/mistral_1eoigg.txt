I have what feels like a nigh-pathological inability to let others know about my failures.

Senior year, my parents pressured me to enroll in their first choice college instead of mine.

My first semester was a fiasco.

This resulted in academic probation.

I hid my failures from my parents, though, saying I'd gotten decent grades.

Sophomore year, I backslid, and my grades were bad enough for the dean to put me on a mandatory semester of leave.

I intercepted the letter that was meant to inform my parents of my suspension, and I led them to believe I was going back for my fourth semester.

I got a 4.0, but my dean balked on some administrative matters and I was told I wouldn't be able to return until the next fall.

I orchestrated my most daring lie yet, which was that I was going to study abroad.

I got great grades, and was accepted back to my original college.

Halfway into the semester I came down with a bacterial infection that wrecked my life for a while.

I ended up failing two classes.

The dean's justice was swift; this time, I couldn't intercept the letter, which informed my parents that I was being put on a year of leave due to my low GPA.

I've made excellent grades at my local college.

No one knows that I attended a total of three semesters at my college.

I want to stay in my home-city, excel in classes, and be around people I love.

I don't know how I can make the transition without revealing the truth to my parents.

I have been consumed by guilt about this for three years, and I just keep escalating my situation.
