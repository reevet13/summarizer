I met her six years years ago at a friend's place and we started dating a couple of months later.

We start talking a lot on the internet and after a couple of months she dumps her boyfriend and tells me she's in love with me.

I soon realize this isn't going to last, since I don't love her the way she loves me.

A couple of months ago (after three years) her boyfriend broke up with her, which - obviously - made her absolutely devastated.

Since they broke up, she's been absolutely devastated and at one point she was almost put into psychiatric care because of her depression.

I should've said something then, but I didn't.

In the meantime I - like most of you should - have realized that I must be in love with her.

I can't stop thinking about the things we could do if we were together.

She never calls, she never messages me and she never takes any initiative to see me.

I'm starting to worry about getting depressed myself because of keeping all of these thoughts and emotions to myself for so long.

She's told me before that she'll never commit suicide, but it's easy to say that when you still think you have something to live for.
