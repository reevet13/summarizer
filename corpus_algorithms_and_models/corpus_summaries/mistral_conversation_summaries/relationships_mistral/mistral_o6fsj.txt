Five months ago, I moved from Upstate New York to Central Coast California, about 3 hours north-west of LA.

That's when she came into my life: 5' 9", Brown hair, Blue eyes, legs for miles, one of the most beautiful women I had ever seen in my life.

A week later we are dating.

My life had become completely surreal, I was living across the country, I was in a relationship, my life had become full of new and exciting things, for the first time in almost 3 years I was truly happy.

After about 3.5 weeks together we were in love.

We would fight at least 3 times a week, sometimes up to 9-10 times a week, about stupid and serious things.

She became depressed, her job search here didn't go well, she couldn't get into any of the local schools, and she let the distance between her and her family get to her.

She is my first true love and I have every intention of marrying her and spending the rest of my life with her.

Last night she decided to move back to California, because she couldn't handle it anymore.

I love her more than anything on the planet.
