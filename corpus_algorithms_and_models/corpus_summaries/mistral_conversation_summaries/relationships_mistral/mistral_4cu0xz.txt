They explained that they had already discussed it at length, and had already started the divorce process.

They promised to keep us informed about all the proceedings they felt would affect us, and were ending it amicably.

I respected him for laying it all out on the table and admitting the mistake was his.

They explained that we were both old enough that a judge would take any choice we made about which parent we wanted to live with into consideration, and that they would as well.

I've decided I want to stay with my dad.

My father is the "Parent" I look up to, mistakes and all.

He's the one who always wakes me up for school, makes my lunch and draws smiley faces on the bag.

He's the one I go to for comfort when I'm upset or sick or scared or just want to talk.

Her definition of helping with homework is yelling at us until we "get it."

Her comforting tactic is to tell us how much harder she had it as a child.

I know she has it in her mind that we'll automatically choose her because my dad was the one that messed up.

I've been through this with her before and she only gives me a choice when she assumes I'll choose what she wants me to.

I'm going to hear about it at every future holiday, every time I see that side of the family, for the rest of my life.

I already know I'll be called a bad child, a bad daughter, that I'm making the wrong choice, that I'm hurting my mother, that I'm a disappointment.

My brother hasn't told them his decision and I'm pretty sure it's because he's waiting to see what I do.

I want to help him stick to his decision, but I know it will only make things worse for me.
