I've all been there: We've all had our hearts torn out, crushed, stepped on, dropped kicked, shredded, and otherwise broken.

The only thing that can heal a broken heart is time.

When you really and truly care for someone, when you really love them, you basically give them a piece of your heart.

When someone takes your love and doesn't offer theirs in return, or stops giving their love, it leaves a hole, or tear, or a broken piece.

The first and most important step is to REMOVE anything that impairs the healing process.

That stuff is called baggage.

The worst heartbreak comes when you've given so much of yourself to someone else that you forget what it's like to just be "you."

This is a perfect opportunity to have a love-affair with yourself.

The most important thing is to get to a point where you're happy with who you are; that you really and truly LOVE yourself.
