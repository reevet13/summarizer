We agreed that, should either of us find someone else, we would make the other aware out of respect, as we both value monogamy.

Almost 6 months later, neither of us have made any move to look for anyone else.

Every boundary we set has been crossed, save the admission that we actually care about each other.

His friends tease us. Our co-workers tease us. My friends tease me. It's almost as though everyone around us has this affirmed knowledge that we are a "couple".

He wants to get married and have kids. He wants his kids to go to Catholic school. He wants his wife to stay at home while he works. He wants her to "fit in" at country clubs and church groups.

I am so comfortable around him, and I feel like it's the same for him.

He goes out of his way to help me, take care of me, and express affection for me.

I can tell he cares more than he's letting on.

Every once in a while, when we've been getting along exceedingly well and are starting to get particularly comfortable, he will remind me that I shouldn't get too close and that our arrangement is temporary.

I'm just truly content with whatever it is we have.

I spend every day waiting for him to drop the bomb that he has found someone else to date.

I'm slowly developing feelings for him.

He treats me like someone he cares deeply for.
