I fell in whatever one would call love at age 11 only two weeks later.

It was honestly as strong as love; it was an absolute understanding that this person was as good as I was ever going to know: something absolutely beyond the normal range of 11 or 12 year old emotions.

I was always aware of his social life, his relationships, and his interests.

He was endlessly kind to me; I am sure I wore my heart on my sleeve, but never made any overt advances.

I spent plenty of time during those years thinking about my future, other boys I wanted to date, and how college would be, but he was always a part of my thoughts and always there in my mind and in my heart.

I certainly did love him and believe him to own a part of me I wasn't ever getting back.

I became much happier in subsequent years of school, having finally found my friend base, and I was able to keep him from my thoughts (although I'll admit that I probably thought or dreamt of him once every week).

I found out through a friend that he was pursuing an endeavor across the country, doing the sort of work I'd dreamt of doing.

I loved the new guy very much, but it wasn't the passionate, reckless, unconditional way I'd loved my first love.

We went out and talked for several hours, reminding me of all the things about him that I loved, and how good he is at his very core.

I'm in the process of getting myself in shape, I've lost nearly 100 pounds and I'm working on the finishing 80.

There is still some of the 12 year old me who believes that he's the other half of me.

I've loved this man for 13 years.
