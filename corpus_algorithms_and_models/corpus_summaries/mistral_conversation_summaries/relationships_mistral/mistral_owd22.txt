Last February my boyfriend broke up with me.

He told me the main reason was that he really wanted to have kids, and I do not.

We'd been together for 2.5 years at that point, living together for 1.5

I spent the rest of the year focusing on myself and my life.

A week and a half ago I got a letter from him asking how I was doing and wondering if we could talk.

We talked for over 2 hours that night and it felt as though the past year hadn't happened, except that we'd both made some positive, much-needed changes in our lives.

I was blown away by how much he'd accomplished.

He apologized for the way he'd acted last February.

He asked if we could meet the next day as he'd be in my town.

It was a little awkward but it was so wonderful to see him again.

I wondered if he intended to ask me if we could try again, and I was surprised to realize that I wouldn't mind that at all.

We agreed that we would plan to see each other on weekends - he wouldn't move back in with me - so we wouldn't fall back into our previous dependent relationship and we'd have our space.

I am ridiculously happy.

He said he'd never stopped thinking of me and everything she did and said he compared with me.

His mom wasn't thrilled for pretty much the same reason, though in her and my bf's father's family there's several instances of younger guy + older woman relationships.

I don't have any big life plans— I've been married and while it may be nice to be so again, I'm not wringing my hands waiting for it.

I guess what I'm trying to get at is acknowledgement that it's okay to be happy with and enjoy what I have right now.

He's planning to become a teacher and has been mentoring and tutoring different age groups and said that he wasn't so sure he really wanted kids after all.
