A three and a half year relationship with the love of my life, things are increasingly, more often painful and stressful than happy and satisfying.

She's truly a beautiful, passionate, intelligent person.

The good times are always bliss.

Lately, I’ve found myself imagining life without her, just to escape the fights.

I have loved this woman from the first and I have never earned her trust.

I love her to the very moment I’m typing this, but at the same time, I feel defeated.

My worldview is shifting tectonic, I’m asking every question and then every other question too.

The problem with questions is, a lot of the times answers are what we want or expect.

I just want to be happy.

I just want her to be happy, but my love, our love is destroying us.

It’s like looking into the eye of a dark chaos.

I've been struggling with my relationship, my thoughts and my emotions for the last few days/months in an unsatisfying fashion.

I react with smug superiority to others' perspectives to feel justified and confident, in order to validate myself, but I'm realizing that it's all just a facade, a house of cards.
