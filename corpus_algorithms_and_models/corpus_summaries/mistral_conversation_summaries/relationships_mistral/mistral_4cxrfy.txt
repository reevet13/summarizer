In February 2015, my girlfriend of just a little over a year and I broke up over a terrible mistake I made.

The guilt from the incident led me to attempt suicide.

We had agreed to no longer have sex and to stop telling one another that we loved each other.

I had received a text from her saying "I need your help. I feel like I am dying."

The next day she was at a party in the same apartment complex I live in and texted me late at night asking if I wanted to have sex.

Over the month of March, things started to seem strange between us.

When I returned to town on a Saturday, I had realized I was blocked on all social media by her.

She then told me that any moment we shared together, she genuinely wished to spend with me.

The next day I left town and dropped the university as I was also failing in my classes (due to consistently dealing with her).

I returned home and was admitted to a mental hospital for a week to help rid myself of the suicidal thoughts that have plagued my mind.

In conclusion I have come to believe that while she had feelings for this guy, she was sleeping with me cause she knows that I would always care about her while waiting to see if this guy reciprocated her feelings.

All I want to do, even as I type this, is talk to her.
