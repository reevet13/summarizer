He and she had been together for six years, and had only stopped seeing/communicating with one another two weeks before the start of the summer program... ie, about three weeks before we slept together.

He confessed to me that he had cheated on his (now officially) ex-girlfriend many, many times.

He said that he felt he was missing something in his relationship with his ex, though regardless of feeling that way, he acknowledged that it did not excuse the cheating and the lying.

I trust him.

Both of us have, in passing, tossed around the idea of possibly living together at some point in the future.

We both want the other to be happy, to be independent and strong, and to pursue our goals and dreams.

If he does indeed pursue the higher education he hopes to, we both understand that we may be too busy to see each other as frequently as we do... too busy to remain in a legitimate relationship.

I have communicated all of my feelings concerning these matters with him, and he has done the same with me.
