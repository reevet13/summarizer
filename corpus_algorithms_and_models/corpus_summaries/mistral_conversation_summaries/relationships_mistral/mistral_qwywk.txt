I met Alma (not her real name, but Alma will do) seven years ago in college.

We only had one class together but we hit it off immediately, we would spend hours talking in the parking lot after class.

We were both single by then but I didn't think it would be worth it to start a long distance relationship.

I dated several girls, some of which I even found more physically attractive than her, but we never lasted more than three weeks.

About eight months ago, she sent me a really nice note for my 26th birthday and it made me think that maybe it just wasn't me who was thinking about what could have been.

She agreed and I went to see her in November.

She told me that she had always felt the same way, and that even though I had waited so long to tell her how I felt, that she wanted to see if there was still something between us.

We started talking on the phone every day.

Things were going great.

She says that she hasn't broken up with him because they share a lot of friends and they all go to class together.

She said she hadn't told me because she was afraid that I would leave her, and that she doesn't want to lose me ever again.

I am still sure that I want to be with her.
