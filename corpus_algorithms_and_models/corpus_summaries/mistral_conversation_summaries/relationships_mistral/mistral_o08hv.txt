I dated this girl for a good 11 months, and during that time I pretty much dedicated 95% of my time, energy, and money to making her happy.

She was my first time, and first girl I ever loved.

A week before homecoming I had a bunch of people over, including X and my other friend because I made it a drama free zone.

The next day I get a call from her telling me that her and X have strong feelings for each other, and I somehow agree to let her take a week to figure out her feelings.

The next day I get a call from another friend of mine around 7 at night saying that her and X were in a relationship on Facebook, I FLIP SHIT.

The next few weeks, the story of what happened gets around really quickly and pretty much everyone is sympathetic for me because they all knew how good of a guy I was and how much I put into the relationship.

All this time my friends who I love forever for accepting me back after blowing them off for a year, are telling me that I shouldn't have to talk to my ex at all.

I repeatedly told her that I didn't want to talk to her at all, and she wouldn't listen.

And then I start to hear that she's having psychological problems and that she was in the hospital for a while because her mom didn't know what to do, and that she's getting medical assistance.

And then I heard that X broke up with her on New Year's Eve.
