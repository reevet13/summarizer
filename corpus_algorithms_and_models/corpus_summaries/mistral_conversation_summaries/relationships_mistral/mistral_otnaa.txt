We moved in together in late 2010.

He was always a bit on the moody side and complain about the world in general.

Early 2011, he started behaving... strange.
He would see underlying meaning in random things, see patterns of behaviour in people that was not acutally there, take responsibility for natural desasters and was sure the world would end.

One day, I found a huge kitchen knife in his drawer, which he used to defend himself against his visionary enemies.

They took him in and put him under lockdown since he could not distance himself from being suicidal.

Back home, he was supposed to take the pills they gave him. He refused, and the symptoms came back.

He claimed they would only make him "like everyone else", fearing he would lose everything he so carefully crafted.

I became emotionally detached.

Slowly, he was getting better.

I told him I wanted him to see a professional. He promised a few times, but never followed through.

Tonight, he told me he felt very alone and that I've not been there for him when he needed me.

He said he wanted to leave me.

I said "Get the fuck out of *my* appartment."

He told me "So, this is who you really are." and I said "I'll tell you who you really are. You never take responsibility for anything."

I know, what I said is not entirely true.

I feel I can no longer support him, since I'm already way beyond my load limit.

A big part of the story is the the probable cause for his illness. Several months before he got sick, I introduced smoking weed into our relationship.

I feel he gave up this stance on drugs to share the experience with me and ended up mentally destroyed because of that.
