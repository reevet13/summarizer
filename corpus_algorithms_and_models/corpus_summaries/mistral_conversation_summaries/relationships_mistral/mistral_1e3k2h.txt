I’m going to visit my bio-dad in about a month for the first time in 5 years.

He survived, without too much damage, but he had several stints put in.

He’s also had a hip replaced, needs the other one done, and couple that with other life-long problems, he’s officially classed as disabled and receives support from the government.

He does volunteer at the SPCA where he lives.

When I phone him, he talks in run-on sentences as if he never gets to talk to anyone (but I know he does).

I have to cut him off to get this important info across, and I feel like such an asshole doing it!

I know he’s old (but not that old), and that he’s not in the best of shape.

I think he may be a bit of a hypochondriac, too.

I’m not sure if he rambles because he’s scared there’ll be awkward silence if he doesn’t, if he fears I won’t have anything to say if he doesn’t talk, or if whatever I do say he won’t be able to relate to or understand.

I just want him to know it’s okay to relax and talk naturally with me, but I feel like I can’t say that because it’ll make him self-conscious or upset and feel like I don’t love him.

I can’t move to where he is because it’s too small and I’d go insane, and there’s no jobs in my professional field out there anyways.

I’m almost all he has, the only person in his life who truly loves him unconditionally and will be there for him for the rest of his life.
