I was officially diagnosed with OCD about a year ago, but I have had the same compulsions for about three or four years. I was offered medication to help suppress my compulsions during stressful times, but I don't have medical insurance and don't plan on accepting the prescription until I am stable enough to get an insurance plan. The symptoms themselves revolve around food. I have a crippling revulsion to solid fat and a small range of other textures. All this has led to other compulsions such as picking apart my food and not being able to eat in the dark since I can't see my food. Even though this sounds really dramatic, I have actually become very well versed in what foods are 'safe' for me and don't set off any of my compulsions. The recent rise in vegetarian and vegan options has actually been amazing for me since knowing they don't have any solid fat has made eating those options effortless. Despite this, since my compulsions have started, I have constantly butted heads with people over my OCD. The first major problem is that some of my friends have watched way too much of 'my strange addiction' and think that if I just face my fears and just eat it, I'll be cured. The second problem has been people just straight up not believing me. Another major problem has been people monitoring my health. My boyfriend is amazing, but he is stuck in this mindset that no one can live without red meat and is constantly hounding me when I choose an alternate protein for my meals. In the end, out of everyone in my life, including my parents, I only have one person who I feel completely safe eating around.
