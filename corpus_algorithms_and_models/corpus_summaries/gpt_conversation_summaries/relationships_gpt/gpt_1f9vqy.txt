For the most part, I would say our marriage is a happy one. I have noticed, however, a pattern that has emerged over the years, and I'm concerned both about the fact that it seems to keep happening and also that the damage it does seems to accumulate over time. The most recent blowup happened this morning. Our son woke up over an hour early on his own, and I had gone to bed late, and so I was downright exhausted when he first woke me up. I knew our kid was simply playing in his room, and I just wanted to muffle the noise, so I got up and closed our bedroom door. My husband's alarm to get up went off a few minutes after that, and the 2nd time he hit the snooze button, I pleaded with him to do me a favor and "do something" to make our son quiet down a bit. He huffs and gets out of bed (which I don't take any issue with), and returns a minute later to try and ride out a few more minutes in bed himself. I get up to close the door, and he immediately jumps out of bed and intercepts me. He shoves me, firmly, away, and declares that he's "already taken care of it" and I can tell that he's fuming. I wanted to clear the air about what happened, so eventually I ask him "can we talk about what just happened this morning?", and he responds by saying "okay, what do you want to talk about?". His response? That he absolutely, didn't shove me. And next thing I know, my husband absolutely LOSES it on me. He just begins screaming at me, hurling insults (calling me a bitch, telling me I'm fat and lazy, etc.). Our poor, sweet, son just does NOT know what to make of his daddy screaming at me like that. Something will trigger a blowup, and next thing I know, I'm hearing months worth of pent-up frustration that I didn't know existed hurled at me all at once, with all of the venom and fury to go with it. And stupidly, I just don't ever see it coming. But, inevitably, time passes and then another blowup happens out of the blue again. Mind you, these are all insults meant for the jugular.
