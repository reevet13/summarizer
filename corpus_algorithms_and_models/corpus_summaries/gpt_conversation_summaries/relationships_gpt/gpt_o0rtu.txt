I was sexually molested at age five. My parents sent me to a private school on Chicago’s North Side, but my mother explicitly told me that I could *not* have friends, because “Boy, people will be one way in front of your face and will tear you down behind your back. People will raise you up to put you down.” My mother would threaten to kill herself or to “get in her car, drive away, and never return” just to watch me become a bawling fucking mess. Since the threat of my parents’ breakup was always an imminent one, my mother could and often did flirt with other men--just to make me cry. My mother was jealous, violent, and confrontational while my father, by contrast, was cool-headed and otherwise aloof and detached. My father spent most of his time avoiding my mother and me. I’d try to greet him with a hug, but he’d usually shove me away. I’d try to wrestle him/hang on him like a jungle gym, and he’d end up really twisting my arm behind my back until I cried from the pain. I spent the rest of that day alone in the apartment, crying. I don’t derive any benefit from being close to others, and it’s been apparent over the years that I’ve burdened would-be friends with my at times all but constant pain. I’m no longer emotionally abusive to my girlfriend at all, and things are mostly fine between us. My girlfriend doesn’t feel like I burden her with my problems.
