Five months ago, I moved from Upstate New York to Central Coast California, about 3 hours north-west of LA. Two days after arriving in California, I was hanging out with a group of my friends at the local In-N-Out and was completely care free, for the first time in years I wasn't depressed and was just having a good time being around people who I had never hung out with in person before. Turns out I was only invited because that beautiful blue eyed girl had told him to invite me, and she had wanted to get to know me. My life had become completely surreal, I was living across the country (I had never lived more than 3 miles from my parents before), I was in a relationship, my life had become full of new and exciting things, for the first time in almost 3 years I was truly happy. I wasn't able to find a job in the area and was running out of money, I had to borrow more money from my parents and my job search didn't look promising. We moved because the town in California had little opportunity for either of us, we had a better chance in my hometown. She became confused, not knowing what she wanted. I unfortunately told her that I couldn't move to California again, there is nothing there for me except for her. I told her that no matter what I loved her and that she was my first true love.
