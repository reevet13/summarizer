Tonight, the boyfriend and I were both drinking.  Yes, we drink on occasion, but just for fun and not in what I consider an unhealthy way.  When we do this, we typically plan our night's dinner to be delivered.  Tonight was no exception - we drank, called in our order to the local cab company (as usual), and waited to get dinner.

When the food arrived, I met the cab in our driveway.  When I presented my credit card the driver flinched and said "I don't have a card reader.  Dammit.  This is the second time they've done this to me tonight."  I knew instantly what he was referring to; usually in my call to the cab company, they ask me if I need someone with a card reader and I always say yes.  Tonight they didn't and it seemed to slip through the cracks.

He told me it was very odd that the cab driver didn't accept credit cards.  Didn't I think this was odd?  I told him no, I've had cabs before who only accepted cash and this was just an anomaly where they sent us one without a card reader.

He kept hammering on the idea that it was suspicious and that every cab driver in town probably had a card reader, that maybe the guy was lying or it was broken and I shouldn't have given him a check.  Growing frustrated at this point, I told him that on the last cab ride I took, I heard the dispatcher specifically asking for drivers WITH card readers, so this anomaly wasn't isolated - other drivers indeed didn't take credit cards.

He kept saying that now the cab driver had all his information (???) and it was very suspicious.  At this point I will admit I lost it and started yelling, saying he was being weird about the whole thing when I had given him very direct and concrete answers about his worries.

I don't want to break up or anything.  I know we can get past this.  I just want to find out if I've done something wrong or if he's in the wrong so I know how to handle stuff like this in the future.
