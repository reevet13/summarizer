Things are fabulous so far (of course they are), but I'm having some trouble loosening up about some things, and I'm worried that I'm either already letting old baggage mess with an otherwise awesome thing, or that maybe the thing itself is too good to be true and that I should be MORE wary.

In the past six years, I've had three major relationships and two incredibly short-lived but explosively damaging flings. To make several long stories short, I've financially supported every man I've ever dated for at least a few months, and usually while I've been juggling school and several minimum wage jobs.

I don't know what I was thinking, but I took him in. I wanted things to get a little more serious, but after a month of staying with me, using my car, eating my food, and spending my money on beer for himself and his buddies, he apparently still wasn't feeling it enough to make things official.

I resolved to stay single for at least a six month period, which I ALMOST accomplished.

I met my current boyfriend, "John" (33), on the internet. I met John in January and knew he was something special right off the bat. We've taken things very, very slow.

John is in a different stage in his life than anyone I've dated. He's a bit older. He's got his own place about a 45 minute drive away from my home, a salaried position at a stable company, and his own needs for alone time and socializing with friends.

I don't know what the hell I could possibly offer him. I'm about to go back to school in August so I'll be taking on (even more) debt.
