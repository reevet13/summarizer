I have been dating my boyfriend for a little more than a year. He’s a smoker and I am a non-smoker. I didn’t realize how bad his habit was until I moved in with him. July of last year I asked him if he ever planned on quitting. He told me that he did plan on quitting and that he would cut back. Though it was hard for me tell how much he cut back because we weren’t around each other 24/7. It SEEMS that he went from 2-3 packs a day to 1 ½ - 2 packs a day by September and then stalled. In November I got him a quit smoking kit, with candies and snacks that could redirect his oral fixation along with a card of encouragement. I asked him to consider a quitting goal date, he never came up with one. I didn’t want to spend the rest of my life with someone who promised to do something, maybe, to only keep me around but not follow through. I love him, but I couldn’t watch him waste away like my great grandfather did from lung cancer he got from smoking. So in mid-February I told him since he hadn’t come up with a quit date, I was. I gave him until April 2nd, and if things didn’t change I was leaving. The month and a half leading up to the date I didn’t see a change in behavior. I smelled smoke, and saw half cigarette stomped out. I went inside without saying anything and started looking for a place to move into. He insisted that it was some clothes of his, or residual smell, that he wasn’t smoking. I pretended that it wasn’t there and asked how he was feeling. He said he felt irritable, forgetful, and his hands were shaking. I told him logically there could only be two explanations, that either he was smoking and he is lying to me about it; or I’m imagining things. My gut tells me that he is lying to me and is willing to go as far as make me think that I’m imagining things (until he came up with his brilliant residual smoke/humidity idea!). I need some objective opinions.
