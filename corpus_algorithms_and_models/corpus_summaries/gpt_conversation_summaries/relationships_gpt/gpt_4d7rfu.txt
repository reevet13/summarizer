As the title says, my room mate "Alex" and I dealt with a bedbug infestation.  If you've ever been through it, you know that the infestation is a nightmare, but the prep is almost worse.  You're basically packing to move.  Clothes have to come out, drawers emptied, furniture moved, it sucks.  And, what's more, no pets can be in the home during treatment.  

Alex's Sil, "Mary", has a brother named "Bob".  They are twins.  Bob is out of town for a family thing for a several weeks, so Mary offered to ask Bob is we could bring the cats to his house while ours was treated.  Bob said that was fine, so Mary gave us the key and we checked the place over.  

Now, this part is important and what I feel is pivotal to the issue:  *We examined the house as it was*.  Any door that was closed, we didn't open.  

Another important point:  *the only way to get to the pool is through the master bedroom, Bob's room*  The door of Bob's room was CLOSED when we looked over the house.  

Alex and I arrive at Bob's house, and Alex proceeds to head to Bob's room to put the mosquito killer into the standing water in the pool.  Kitty and Cat are following behind.  

I investigate and find..... FLEAS!!!  All over the bathroom floor.  All over the rug in Bob's room.  Crawling up my legs (I had shorts on).  On Alex's pants.  *There. Are. Fleas. Everywhere!!!*

We immediately run to get Seresto collars for the cats and slap them on them.  We aren't going to swap fleas for bedbugs.  No way, no how, and the cats don't need those irritating, disease carrying vectors on them, either.  

The next day, Alex asks Mary if Bob might be willing to pay for the collars, or at least part of them.  

Mary *absolutely freaks out*.  She went from zero to murderous rage in one text message.  She said "You CHOSE to take them there, you looked over the house and said it was fine, we did this as a favor to family and I can't believe that you would think we owe you anything!"  

We don't get it.  We do not get it at all.  We made no accusation.  We understand that Bob probably didn't know about the fleas in the house
