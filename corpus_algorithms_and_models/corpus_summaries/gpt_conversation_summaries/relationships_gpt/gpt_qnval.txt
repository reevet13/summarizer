A three and a half year relationship with the love of my life, things are increasingly, more often painful and stressful than happy and satisfying. I was seized by my love for her three months after we met. I *knew,* with every ounce of my heart and mind that I had met my soul mate. She's truly a beautiful, passionate, intelligent person. The good times are always bliss. I have dreamed ceaselessly about our future together, earning a very good income, having exactly two kids, one boy, one girl. Lately, I’ve found myself imagining life with out her, just to escape the fights. Furious, violent, ancient disagreements, like worlds colliding, creating the rubble of the next universe. I have loved this woman from the first and I have never earned her trust. I love her to the very moment I’m typing this, but at the same time, I feel defeated. I’ve tried, she’s tried, we’ve struggled to understand each other. My worldview is shifting tectonic, I’m asking every question and then every other question too. I am also learning how to be wrong. I just want to be happy. I just want her to be happy, but my love, our love is destroying us. I've been struggling with my relationship, my thoughts and my emotions for the last few days/months in a unsatisfying fashion. In any serious relationship, I think if we commit ourselves to another person as honestly and openly as possible, we expose ourselves to be judged and to be held to account by them.
