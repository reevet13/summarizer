You see, once upon a time I was the youngest child of an older couple. Years went by and while I was in college, my father passed away unexpectedly. Surprised and sad, I stopped college and took a job in her town. I moved in with her (with her permission) and spent my next two years in horror learning just how deep the rabbit hole went. And the worse yet was that the rabbit was none other than the middle sibling, someone 11+ years older than me. Although she wanted me to fix things, particularly the money problems; she only wanted me to fix them in so much as they would not affect my sibling. I'll say that I went to the school of sudden and hard knocks. For this particular rabbit was not done trying to get to wonderland. She was the enabler and, as it came down to it, the rebel against me. Time played out. My rabbit hopped the country, much to my pleasure. But it happened again, in a scheme I had once again never heard of: check-kiting. Sadly, I sobered up and realized that I may never be free of keeping a wary eye on my mother at this rate. Today, however, is the tale of my trying to save my mother again from my sibling. I let my sibling know that I would take out a guardianship to protect my mother with the information from their previous scams if the sibling didn't start paying my mother back or make the mooch leave. I love her, despite it all, and I can see how much of her behavior is a response to how she feels about losing my father even this far after my father's death.
