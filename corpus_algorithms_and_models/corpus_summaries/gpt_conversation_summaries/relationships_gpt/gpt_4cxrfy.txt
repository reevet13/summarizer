In February 2015, my girlfriend of just a little over a year and I broke up over a terrible mistake I made. One day she found out, and we effectively broke up over it all. After my stay in the hospital, we talked things through where I genuinely apologized for what happened. From that day on, we would still hang out and do everything we usually did and tell each other that we love each other. However, when anyone would ask about our relationship status, we would tell people we weren't dating.

Eventually, in November we would have a sit down conversation where we both agreed we needed to actually breakup. Both of us had agreed that the other was too important in the others life to let go and we had wished to stay friends. The next day she was at a party in the same apartment complex I live in and texted me late at night asking if I wanted to have sex. So we did. From then on we fell back into our old pattern, minus saying we loved the other.

When I returned to town on a Saturday, I had realized I was blocked on all social media by her. I had called her out on it all and she still didn't respond. So I had attempted to commit suicide after telling her where she could find my body. She promised me that she never intended to hurt me like she did and just didn't know how to tell me. I returned home and was admitted to a mental hospital for a week to help rid myself of the suicidal thoughts that have plagued my mind.
