Both my boyfriend (let's call him H) and I (I am a female) turned 21 this year, and have been together for over over thirteen months. We unfortunately decided to take all the same classes last year, which led to a break-up in November. H was overwhelmed and felt very smothered by me-- I can be very clingy at my worst, and I was definitely in a bad place in late 2011. H's friend committed suicide earlier in that year and my parents were/are going through a horrible divorce. Thankfully, we reunited, worked things out, and things were pretty awesome for the past few months...until the last few weeks transpired. H and I have a very open, loving, experimental relationship- not in the poly-amorous sense, but more in the areas of drug-culture and now sexuality. Now that we were more open physically, we both decided to try MDMA together; he had used before, this was my first time. The MDMA crash is infamous for being rough, riddled with depression and a lack of serotonin. H struggled with this harder than I did, and I'm not entirely sure he ever recovered. Fights started popping up again, for no reason. H told me he was "extremely unhappy with the way the relationship has been" and that although he "loves me and wants to be with me" that he doesn't know if he can be in a relationship right now. When we finally talked, things made a little more sense but were still fairly vague—H says he feels like our relationship can’t change unless we break up, and that he’s very confused with who he is and what his identity is. He also told me he doesn’t think we can grow up unless we are apart and that he feels like I’ll maintain my abandonment complex and he’ll constantly remain feeling inadequate unless we take separate. I’ve only really talked about the bad, since that is what I am experiencing, but in general, H is one of the most supportive, caring, talented and incredible people I have ever met.
