I moved for grad school and instantly met a guy who I had a really great connection with. After about 6 months we decided to change our close friendship into an EXCLUSIVE FWB situation. We thought there would be no harm because we were so close, but did not feel romantically inclined toward one another. Flash forward however and about 5 months after we had been hooking up, we both talked about having more significant feelings for one another. We basically decided that we both had romantic and emotional feelings for each other, so we would just continue to be in an exclusive relationship but that we would not be bf/gf. Recently, after a night where he came over just because he missed me and we just cuddled and watched tv and fell asleep, he went out the next night and cheated on me. I was completely blindsided and crushed. I decided that if it was what we both wanted, we could still work to be friends, even though we knew that would be hard. We decided we would work on being friends, but I realized that I was the only one really putting in any effort. I hate how everything ended and I hate how things are and while I know that with everything that has happened the romantic part of us needs to be over, I never wanted our friendship to be over too. I feel like there has been a death of a friend and I am left with such an empty space in my life.
