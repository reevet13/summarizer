Two years ago I met a guy. I fell quick, and hard for him. After hooking up, and hanging out for about a month he broke things off with me. Eight months ago he refriended me. Six months ago we start talking pretty regularly. We talked about what would be different this time, that neither of us were looking for a relationship, that there'd be a mutual respect of boundaries, I made it clear that what mattered most to me was our friendship and that if this was going to impede that in anyway I wasn't interested. After that night I decided not to text him, not to call, to let him initiate anything just as to not risk a repeat of being as overly attached as I'd been the year before. He's texting me every day, and I thought it was all good. Then I get a text from him saying he feels "guilty" that he thinks casual sex is preventing him from working on his relationship issues, and that this hooking up is making him feel like a jerk. He expresses he's really appreciative of my understanding, and he wants to stay friends. I thought for sure that that would be the catalyst to sex again, but when it doesn't happen I write it off and figure we really are just friends at this point. I end up going over. The next night I ask to see him and he gives me the "guilty" talk again. We talk about our fears, our first loves, about sex, about family, about God, about books, about everything you can imagine. I get a text from him "Thanks for all the help" and I tell him it's not a problem.
