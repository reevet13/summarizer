My boyfriend (let's call him Mike) and I met in college and immediately hit it off - we have a lot in common, became best friends and the sex is incredible. Our problems began after we graduated. We both had a lot of trouble finding jobs, which put us under a lot of pressure - but eventually I managed to be offered a fantastic graduate job in my field (engineering) where I worked for almost three years, getting promoted and doing really well. In the meantime, however, he had been struggling. About two years ago, after seeing how miserable he was, I told Mike that if he really wants to work on his writing, I would be happy to pay for most of our rent/food/etc. for him so he didn't have to do a full time job. Six months ago, I was offered a new job in a different city (about five hours drive away) with a salary that was about ten times what I made in my graduate role. Mike did, however, tell me that he was tired of me having to pay for everything for us, so before joining me in this new city he wanted to move back in with his parents and start applying for full time jobs in new city and be long distance until he found work, then could move over with me. Six months passed, and suddenly Mike tells me he is very unhappy over Skype one day. I said if he feels like he can't do long distance anymore, why doesn't he just come to the new city to be with me? Mike tells me he would be happy to move right away, but he doesn't want a "shitty" part time job and will only come over to live with me if I pay for him to move, then continue paying 100% for both of us to live here until he finds a full time job that he enjoys. He insists he would do the same for me if the situation was reversed. He then also tells me "what's the point of me coming all the way to new city if after a few years you'll just leave me again to go somewhere else?" and is telling me "people make deeper promises every day when they get married, and you won't even do this for me".
