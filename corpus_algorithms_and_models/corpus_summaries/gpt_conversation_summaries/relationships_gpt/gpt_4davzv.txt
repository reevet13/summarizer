Both my dad and I allow too much anger to build up over time, and because of our inability healthily express our anger release it all at once in a hurricane of rage. While he has never been physically abusive whenever he decides to explode he becomes quite verbally abusive, and tends to resort to taking shots at people below the belt including me for my entire life. I hoped that things would get better once I moved out to go to college in the fall of 2014. At first things between us improved, but once I became comfortable at school and returned home less and less things became unbearable again. He tells me how I am a stupid fuck up (an assumption made about me since he was a stupid fuck up at my age), and constantly makes fun of me for my career goals. This would all be bearable though if it were not for the way that he treats my mom and my brother. Just a few weeks ago I went home for spring break. "Hey son. It's nice to see you." Pauses. "It would be nice to be able to talk to you more, but I expect you to make the effort to call me. I shouldn't have to make that effort. That's all on you." Awkward silence. For nearly a month now I have had absolutely zero contact with my father. Not a call. Not a text. Not an email. I haven't even asked for him when he is sitting right next to my mom while I talk to her and he knows who she is talking to, and to be honest I couldn't be happier.
