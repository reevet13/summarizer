I ran into Bob a couple times early in the year. I'm in town only for a few months, so I don't know a lot of people in the building. Oh, also, this is a platonic story. I’m a straight guy and Bob at least claims to have a girlfriend.

Anyway, Bob. Bob lives in my apartment complex, and I borrowed him my monitor for a couple weeks (I use a laptop for work, so not having a dual display didn’t kill me). I don’t ask for anything back, but Bob brought me some food returning the monitor and we talk for a bit. He’s been working on some projects, and thinks he has some potential to make money. He seems like a smart guy. A couple weeks later we bump into each other at a supermarket, and I ask him if he wants to grab a beer later.

He gets a couple more drinks out of me, alternately giving me the impression that he thinks he’s tricking me, or that he will be generous when he comes into his power. He needs constant affirmations from me verifying that he is right, or that I’m impressed etc... At this point I’m just coasting and morbidly curious.

He won’t stop knocking, and he wants me around all the time, wants to meet my girlfriend when she comes to visit, tells me he’s going to make me rich and I should quit my job.

I would rather make him decide I’m boring, and just be as generally ambivalent to me as he is to 99% of the population. I want to fall off the radar. I want to be boring, disappointing, and not worthy of his company. I want the measure of his vengeance to be denying me his presence. Crystal?
