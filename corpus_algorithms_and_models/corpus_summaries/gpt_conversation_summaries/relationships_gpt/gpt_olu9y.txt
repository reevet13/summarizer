He recently came out to me, informing me I am the only person to ever know of his secret and that I will be the only one to know for ages to come. We dated for a few months at the beginning, and on more than one occasion we have both referred to these months as *the happiest of our lives*. Eventually, we started talking again, and became closer than ever. I put my feelings away in a drawer I did not allow myself to peek at. After two years of battling myself, I finally manned up and ended my facade. He told me that often when he laid in bed at night he thought of me. He told me I would never lose him and that he knew now that we two needed to be in each other's lives. It was not til weeks later that he told me he'd gotten my letter, poured over it millions of times, memorized it. Even though we agreed to not make things serious and just take them as they'd come, we began flirting and talking almost every day. He told me that for as long as he could remember, he'd been gay. He told me I was the only girl ever to convince him that perhaps he'd been wrong, but no, as soon as things started to get physical, he knew we could not be. I want to be his true friend, be there for him for the hell he must be going through, yet I cannot help but cry at my misfortune.
