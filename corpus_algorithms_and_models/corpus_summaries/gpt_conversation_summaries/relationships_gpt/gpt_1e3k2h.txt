My bio-parents were divorced when I was about 6. I always kept in touch with my bio-daddy, even though I didn’t get to see him much. I’m going to visit my bio-dad in about a month for the first time in 5 years. In 2009, while I was out of the country and approximately 8000 km away from him, he had a heart attack. He survived, without too much damage, but he had several stints put in. He’s also had a hip replaced, needs the other one done, and couple that with other life-long problems, he’s officially classed as disabled and receives support from the government. He does volunteer at the SPCA where he lives. He fosters cats, and helps take care of them and clean out their cages. Still, sometimes I feel like he’s still a little too ‘shut-in.’ When I phone him, he talks in run-on sentences as if he never gets to talk to anyone (but I know he does). I’m not the most patient person, admittedly, but this is very hard to deal with. Last month it was his birthday, and I tried to phone him three times on the day. I had to work that night, so I couldn’t try any more. I know he’s old (but not that old), and that he’s not in the best of shape. I think he may be a bit of a hypochondriac, too. I just want him to know it’s okay to relax and talk naturally with me, but I feel like I can’t say that because it’ll make him self-conscious or upset and feel like I don’t love him.
