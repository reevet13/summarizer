Ultimately, awards for things as subjective as books are based on _opinion_ and _politics_. 

The Pulitzer board thought Gravity's Rainbow was "unreadable, turgid, overwritten, and obscene." The first three are all the same thing. It's a matter of opinion.

Great opinion pieces are written every year.  If the Board gives up on deciding which piece deserves the highest recognition, they're not trying hard enough.  To some it makes them look pompous, or lazy, or tightwads.  And that can't be good for the Pulitzer brand.
