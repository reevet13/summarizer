Title is misleading. The Mint is rationing silver coins that it creates, not silver itself. It's a matter of too much demand for not enough coin making supply, not too much demand for silver itself.  
If you're out and about you should be able to buy bullion silver coins. They're a far better deal than silver rounds, be it eagles or maple leafs. Plus a big bonus, this worn old coinage is far harder to counterfeit than the current "investment" mint product.  
There are hundreds of millions of ounces of silver squirreled away in dresser drawers all over the USA. Nearly all those Kennedy half dollar pieces were collected, not spent.  
Seriously, a lot of stuff was melted when the Hunt brothers tried to corner the market.  
Another source of silver is jeweler's supplies.  
Another source to consider is Craig's List. There are pages and pages of people selling their jewelry, and from the prices they're asking, I am presuming that many sellers are not aware of the scrap value of their gold and silver.  
This market is just right for counterfeits if buyers are this desperate. Most people, buying these coins for the first time, may not realize they've just purchased a slug of white jeweler's metal covered with a thin silver flash coat.  
Just a heads up. Only buy silver from a licensed dealer.
