The decoding bit is phenomenal -- in part because it's very ingenious, and in part because no one would EVER conclude that the symbol represents the letters N and D, standing for Nuclear Disarmament.  
Someone told me once that it was a "broken cross" and was therefore a sign of the devil and should never be used. Can you believe I was told this in a southern baptist church? (*shocker*)  
I think it has to do with medieval bowmen using their middle finger to draw their bows. When an army was defeated, to make the bowmen useless they'd cut off the middle finger. I suppose showing your middle finger was a way of saying "I can still kill you!"  
I used to believe this too, but apparently it's an [urban legend](http://www.icons.org.uk/theicons/collection/the-v-sign/biography/the-anti-french-gesture).  
When I was in private school, we were not allowed to wear peace signs because they were "a satanic symbol". They said it was a picture of the cross with the arms broken.  
I was raised being taught it was a communist symbol showing their disbelief of God and Jesus Christ because it was an upside down, broken cross.
