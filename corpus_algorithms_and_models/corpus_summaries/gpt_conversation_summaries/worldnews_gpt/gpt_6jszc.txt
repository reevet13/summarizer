This does seem like a rather one-sided article with suspicious intentions.
Denmark is the OECD country with the biggest difference between unemployment for immigrants and non immigrants. For immigrants unemployment is a lot higher.
The source of the statistics is a New York post article by [Daniel Pipes](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Pipes).
You can read the original article and two followups [here](http://www.danielpipes.org/article/450).
The article was originally published in the New York Post as "Something Rotten in Denmark" on August 27, 2002, by Daniel Pipes and Lars Hedegaard.
The rape statistic is hard to pin down, but reasonable. The Minister of Justice in Denmark reported that 76.5% of the rapes in Copenhagen were committed by non-native Danes, and over 3/4 of non-native Danes are Muslim. However, there are no nationwide statistics, and official police records do not record religion.
The welfare quote is more difficult to swallow considering Denmark offers many "welfare" services to all citizens such as health care. They must have meant some specific category of welfare. But the original article (which comes from a relatively credible source . . . but not the best) does say "upwards of 40% of welfare spending."
