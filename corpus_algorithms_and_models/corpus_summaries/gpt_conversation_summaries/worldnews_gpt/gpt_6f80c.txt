The pics are from 1993. The sign says premeditated murder and her name: "Lu Jinfeng" (陆金风). She was a 19 year old girl from Shijiazhuang in Hebei province and was sentenced to death for poisoning her husband. Dunno bout those other dudes.  
I looked at these exact same photos a few years back and asked a Chinese friend to give me the low-down. He found one article in Chinese about it which said on that day a woman was executed for killing her husband and a man and a woman were executed for trafficking children. It didn't say anything about the rest, I think it was just explaining why two women were being executed (even in China women make up less than 1% of those executed, so it's fairly noteworthy when it's a woman catching the bullet).
