Just a coupla minutes of gentle shaking of the building. If buildings are shaking in Beijing then it must be a lot worse at the epicenter. I read that the epicenter is in Chengdu, i hope that it didn't hit them hard. Aftershocks are expected to hit Beijing from 10 pm to midnight. The strength of the shocks are expected to be from 2 to 6 on the Richter Scale. Chengdu is like 1000mi / 1600km away from here, so to feel it is pretty serious. The epicenter was near the eastern edge of Tibet and nowhere near any Olympic venues (which are along the coast). I'm worried it may not look too good once the whole situation unfolds - the epicenter is at wenchuan, which is an area where the majority of the population is minorities, and their dwellings are old-school brick houses.
