Apparently some Asians simply don't have the enzyme to properly process alcohol. I get headaches and a really flushed face instead of feeling drunk - it's a real downer..
[Alcohol flush reaction](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_flush_reaction)

Essentially, their acetaldehyde dehydrogenase is weaker than in other people, so they cannot process acetaldehyde (which the body metabolizes from alcohol, and is 30 times more toxic) as quickly. It accumulates and makes them feel sick.

To my experience, as an asian (and thus in the "asian community"), we don't drink nearly as much - at least in quantity, if not in regularity. Part of it's genetic. Part of it is also cultural, at least for 2nd generation asians in the US.
