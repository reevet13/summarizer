This really is the biggest conflict of the last 50 years. Some reports show more than 5 million killed, making it the biggest conflict since world war II.  
Actually, the Congo is extraordinarily rich in natural resources, including diamonds, cobalt, uranium, and the tantalum used in many electronics.  
The riches of the Congo have been its curse since the days of Belgium rule, when the rubber craze fueled one of the most brutal and devastating colonial regimes ever.  
Mining continues throughout the fighting, and the money it generates goes back into the conflict.  
The Belgian colonial occupation of the DRC was one of the most brutal ever undertaken, during which around 10 million people died from mistreatment and disease; the population was literally reduced by half.  
The democratically elected leader of the free DRC, Patrice Lumumba, lasted a very short time in office, in large part because of the international panic his leftist views incited; his kidnapping and assassination were accomplished with the direct involvement of the Belgian military and the monetary support of the US.  
The involvement of many neighboring countries, who have their own reasons to be interested in the mineral wealth of the eastern Congo, has prolonged the conflict and provide arms and manpower.
