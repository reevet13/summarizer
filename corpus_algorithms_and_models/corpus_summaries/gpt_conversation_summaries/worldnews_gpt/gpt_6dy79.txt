Lasers suck. We need to get rid of all lasers now while we have a chance. In the future, punk kids with deadly DIY green lasers will be causing jet planes to catch fire and crash, and burning houses and buildings from a quarter mile away. We will be almost powerless to stop them as they reduce our Earth to ash.  
The new range of green lasers available (eg http://www.thinkgeek.com/gadgets/lights/5a47/) are significantly more powerful than the red ones traditionally used in lectures/seminares  
Even if the laser was coherent enought at a range of a few hundred meters to hurt someone even the smallest vibration in the wielder's hand would be enough to move the far end of the beam many meters. The idea that you could shine it on a target with a 3mm radius like the pupil of an eye - through a freaking *plane* - for long enough to do damage is just ridiculous.  
I don't think it's the damage to the eye thing that's so bad - I think it's more that the pilots are distracted by it.  
That being said, temporary flash blindness and the startle reflex could easily cause a pilot to abort a landing.  
There's a difference between hitting the plane at all and getting a perfect shot right in the window. Especially from the ground.
