The rules of congress need to be changed to make anonymous votes illegal(the pdf states the bill passed on an anonymous voice vote). How are we supposed to hold government accountable if we have no idea who voted for this legislation? 

Second of all, this is plainly and simply a violation of our rights. Our government is not authorized to simply take a copy of our genetic material, and I pray the supreme court will strike this law down(which, unfortunately, I doubt)

An anonymous study of population genetics might be tolerable for better research purposes, but this genetic branding of every individual born in the united states (how long before they ask it of travelers and citizens too?) crosses the line. Especially since it's opt-out (but who knows about it?) and it allows the government to keep track of 'defective' persons. I'm not advocating the slippery slope theory here but with the apathy of the american public in general I could see a lot of potential abuse of this kind of information, not limited to genetic discrimination and national health care 'emergencies' where the government tries to obtain the right to dictate a group's life based on their genetic map. It's about as close as true racism (genetism?) would get. Scary...

Newborn testing is already in place in every state. States decide which disorders to test for, and they are generally rare metabolic disorders (enzyme deficiencies, etc.) that can be managed with dietary restrictions. Most of the disorders can be fatal if not diagnosed in the first few days or weeks of life.  

In the end, is it more important that your kid be omitted from a database, or that your kid lives beyond a week?

There is a difference between performing a one-time screening and establishing a mandatory, nation-wide genetic database.
