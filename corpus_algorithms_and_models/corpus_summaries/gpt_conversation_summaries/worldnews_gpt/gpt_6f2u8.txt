They've also paid for that infusion with their lives. The last Tibetan uprising 49 years ago saw around 80 thousand Tibetans killed. Numbers are never known because the Communist government will not release them. The generally understood figure is around 800,000 Tibetans have been killed or have *gone missing* since Chinese rule 60 years ago. During the Cultural Revolution much of Tibet's temples were smashed, burned, the treasures stolen and broken, it was the raping of a culture. Their leader has been forced to live in exile, and attempts to aid the refugees that risk their lives crossing the border into Nepal and then to India. Hundreds of people flee Tibet every year. In Daramsala they get taught about their culture, their history, their language, for the new generation often for the first time in their lives.
