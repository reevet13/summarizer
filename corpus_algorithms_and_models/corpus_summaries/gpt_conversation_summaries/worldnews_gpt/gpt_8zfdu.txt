This is Cynthia McKinney and I’m speaking from an Israeli prison cellblock in Ramle. While we were on our way to Gaza the Israelis threatened to fire on our boat, but we did not turn around. The Israelis highjacked and arrested us because we wanted to give crayons to the children in Gaza. During Operation Cast Lead, US-supplied F-16s rained hellfire on a trapped people. I was later told by doctors who were there in Gaza during Israel’s onslaught that Gaza had become Israel’s veritable weapons testing laboratory, people used to test and improve the kill ratio of their weapons. The Israeli authorities have tried to get us to confess that we committed a crime... I am now known as Israeli prisoner number 88794. I am facing deportation from the state that brought me here at gunpoint after commandeering our boat. I am being held in this prison because I had a dream that Gaza’s children could color and paint, that Gaza’s wounded could be healed, and that Gaza’s bombed-out houses could be rebuilt. They are refugees from tragedy, and they made it to Israel, only after they arrived Israel told them, "There is no UN in Israel." The police here have license to pick them up and suck them into the black hole of a farce for a justice system. The truth is that Israel lied to the world. Israel lied to the families of these young women. I appeal to the United Nations to get these women of Ramle, who have done nothing wrong other than to believe in Israel as the guardian of the Holy Land, resettled in safe homes. I dedicate this message to those who struggle to achieve a free Palestine, and to the women I’ve met at Ramle.
