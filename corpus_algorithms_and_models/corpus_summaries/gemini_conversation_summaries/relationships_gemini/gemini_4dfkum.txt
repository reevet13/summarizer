It seems as if my mother still holds it against me that I moved out over a year ago.
My mother and I have never been close since I was a small child.
She was visibly annoyed when I got my results, and the last time I felt any genuine display of pride from her was when I picked up my GCSE results.
During this time she bought me my first car, which I was very grateful for.
She then decided that I owed her the £50 a week Jobseekers Allowance I was entitled to as rent money.
After a year of living there, feeling as if I was resented and now having no money, I decided to move out.
When I got home, she cried and shouted about how she was losing me, how could I not tell her this etc etc.
When she was out one day, I opened her laptop to use it and found it open on her Facebook page, with messages to her sister and friend open and my name mentioned.
Since I read those messages I haven't really trusted anything she says.
She threw a fit that I moved out the weekend of Mother's Day, and did not help me move a single thing or let me keep anything apart from my clothes and personal belongings.
I have never ever felt so unwelcome in anyone's house as that day and haven't been back since.
I did not handle the above situation very well, and after the barrage of angry texts I received from her I just ignored her.
I've apologised to her a few times for what I have done wrong, but when I brought up how she had upset me all I got was "I don't remember saying those things, I must have been very upset and people say things they don't mean when they are upset.
