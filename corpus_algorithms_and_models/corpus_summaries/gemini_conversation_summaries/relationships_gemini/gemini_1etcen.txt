I feel very reassured in deciding to cut these people out of my life for good.
Genuine people don't shy away from owning their mistakes or at least discussing the issue with the person they have a "problem" with.
Eventually, it got so bad I had to stop speaking to them because they had NO respect for the fact I was attached and very happy.
Throughout the whole process, my boyfriend has been wonderfully supportive, and did not like seeing me treated poorly; he sat me down and told me it might be wise to take a step back and really ask myself if these were the kinds of people I wanted to try to keep a relationship with (and by that, he meant people who didn't seem to value my friendship).
Recently, I met with one of the people I used to be close to in that group, and was told that they all resented our relationship because they had apparently lost me because of my boyfriend, ie: he "took me away".
No one ever expressed that they felt I was neglecting them, and frankly, I don't think I was, given I was visiting them a few times a week and still actively trying to include everyone in plans I made despite the lack of effort from them.
But the people I used to call my friends?
They still refuse to even speak to me (with the exception of a few, but they won't address the issue), I get dirty looks and am snubbed by some of them in passing, etc. and so I finally lost it tonight and I removed all of them from my contacts and social media after what I feel was a wasted year and a half of one-sided attempts to maintain these friendships.
