My boyfriend and I have been together for almost 5 years, living together for most of that time.
Things have been pretty good up until the last year, when shit hit the fan.
And all this has thrown our relationship on the rocks.
Life has gotten less stressful overall, but the relationship continues to degrade.
I feel good about life again, not about this relationship.
Counselor says I've done everything I can to express my needs (more financial contribution, more help with housework, and most important of all - more emotional support), and at this point I need to see how far he'll go and whether I can accept what he offers.
He won't see a relationship counselor.
We have repetitive conflicts over stupid things that are summed up pretty well by the exchange below, and in the end I always end up feeling lonely, neglected, and like how I feel doesn't matter.
After our latest fight last night, I decided to write him a letter.
I thought it might be a good way for us to sort things out without ending up in yet another argument.
Please tell me what you think....
I felt hurt when I asked you for help and you accused me of being passive aggressive.
I felt confused when later you said I didn't ask you for help, when I did.
Then I felt hurt again when you called me a jerk.
I was tired, I hate cleaning, I was cranky.
I'm sorry about that.
In the past I would've just apologized and made nice and moved on, without expressing how I felt.
I promised myself that this would change, because I don't want to end up depressed again, and the depression was the result of me keeping my feelings to myself for far too long, as if they didn't matter.
That's why I wrote this letter.
The way you responded is NOT an excuse for me being cranky.
I apologize for being cranky.
But it doesn't change that I was hurt and confused by the way you acted.
If you want to learn from this, then write back.
If you don't want to do this, don't do it.
Don't do it half-assed or sarcastically.
Either you genuinely want to learn from this or you don't.
If you do: Tell me how things happened from your perspective.
Tell me what you could have done differently, in hindsight.
How you could've reacted differently that might've been less hurtful.
Tell me how you would've wanted me to do things differently.
Please don't dig into the past.
Don't say "You always..." or "You are..." Just focus on this incident.
What am I doing wrong here?
Am I being a jerk?
Is he being a jerk?
If all of our conflicts end like this...I don't understand how we'll ever recover :( What I would've loved was for him to say something like "I'm sorry I got pissy about you being pissy.
I could've just helped out." What I feel like I got is "Here are all the things you did wrong.
Don't be cranky.
Stop assuming I don't want to help.
The end."
