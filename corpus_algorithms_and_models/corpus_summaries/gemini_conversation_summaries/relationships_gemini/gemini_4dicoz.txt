My dad (60s) and I (late 20s) have had a tumultuous relationship over the years.
Looking back, there was a lot of ambient abuse and dysfunction in our house, but on the other hand, in so many ways he's been inspirational and encouraging to me.
There are plenty of things I admire about him, but for much of my life, I've had to play the role of parent to him, and be the voice of reason, sanity, stability.
I've recently returned back to my hometown in order to work on a project utilizing space available at my mom's house.
This sends me into a spiral of anxiety and guilt that makes it hard to breathe, frankly.
I care about him and I value continuing to try to have a good relationship with him.
But when I imagine the possibility that, in a few years, I'll be the only one nearby to take care of him, if his health were to decline, that I would be practically expected to, who knows, move in with him and be his caretaker.
I would have to give up everything I'm working so hard for just as I feel my life is about to take off...
I just can't do it.
But I just can't give up my life for that...
