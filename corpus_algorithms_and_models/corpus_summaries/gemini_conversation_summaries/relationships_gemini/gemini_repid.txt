So, I couldn't help but notice the rise in "How do I get over them" posts and I thought I'd share some of my thoughts and experience with the heartbroken members of Reddit.
We've all sat in the dark, listened to love ballads or R&amp;B, and rage rock or gangsta rap, while looking at their Facebook profile, checking their updates or recently posted pics, or whatever.
And after all my experience with women and dating, I think I've come up with a fool proof way to get over your heartache: **TIME**

The only thing that can heal a broken heart is time.
Well, the first and most important step is to REMOVE anything that impairs the healing process.
All those photos, cards, letters, texts, emails, videos, Facebook comments; those are like shrapnel in a broken heart.
This is a perfect opportunity to have a love-affair with yourself.
The most important thing is to get to a point where your happy with who you are; that you really and truly LOVE yourself.
