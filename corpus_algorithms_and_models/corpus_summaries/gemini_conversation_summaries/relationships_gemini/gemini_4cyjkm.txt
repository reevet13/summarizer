I was officially diagnosed with OCD about a year ago, but I have had the same compulsions for about three or four years.
The symptoms themselves revolve around food.
If I consume it or even think I consumed it, I'll get severely nauseated, no longer be able to eat, and sometimes throw up if the exposure is too large.
All this has led to other compulsions such as picking apart my food and not being able to eat in the dark since I can't see my food.
The only thing I ask out of my relationships when it comes to food is that when we go out that we don't go out to joints that focus on my super big problem foods like hamburgers or sausage.
Despite this, since my compulsions have started, I have constantly butted heads with people over my OCD.
The first major problem is that some of my friends have watched way too much of 'my strange addiction' and think that if I just face my fears and just eat it, I'll be cured.
The second problem has been people just straight up not believing me.
Another major problem has been people monitoring my health.
My boyfriend is amazing, but he is stuck in this mindset that no one can live without red meat and is constantly hounding me when I choose an alternate protein for my meals.
In the end, out of everyone in my life, including my parents, I only have one person who I feel completely safe eating around.
It is making me extremely self conscious and means that I have been refusing to eat out or in people's homes in lieu of just cooking myself something before an event to avoid problems.
The problem is, eating around my OCD is so normal for me, I don't know if I am just being delusional and I really shouldn't expect the concessions I get for my OCD, especially since I chose not to medicate, even though I don't know if the compulsions would be completely repressed by medication or not.
