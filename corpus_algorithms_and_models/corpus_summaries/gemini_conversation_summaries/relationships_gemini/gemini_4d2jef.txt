My girlfriend (Sarah) and I have been dating for almost three years.
Marriage has been seriously discussed, and I'd like to get engaged before the end of 2016.
However, there's one problem that we've had throughout our relationship...she hasn't felt welcomed in my family by my parents.
Sarah's parents, who live about 30 minutes away from us, are extremely nice and kind, and have welcomed me into their family with open arms since day one, for which I'm incredibly grateful.
My family is definitely a bit more subdued than hers are, and not quite as outward with our emotions.
Sarah's interaction with my parents, more specifically, my mom, haven't been positive.
This whole experience has been difficult for me for a variety of reasons.
Therapy made me realize that I don't do a good enough job seeing a problem through Sarah's eyes; rather, I see her problem through my eyes, and how I would react to it.
Knowing this, I began to take a harder line with my parents in terms of having them treat Sarah better.
My mom responded to me that she had no idea what I was talking about, and that the reason she never called or texted Sarah was that "she wasn't sure if she was the kind of person who would be okay with that."
