I have been living a few hours away from my parents' home for the past four years due to university, but when I come home for holidays or family events I have noticed that my grandmother takes every opportunity to berate me- my choice of degree, my eating habits, my social life, my friends, and anything else that you can imagine.
With hindsight she has always behaved in this way, but I have really started to notice it after a period of living away from home.
My mum is no small woman, but she is certainly no more than 200lbs, and stands at around 5ft 8; this constant low level criticism from my grandmother is something that has always rattled my mum for as long as I can remember, especially as she has struggled with her weight since her early teens.
Grandmother has always been the sort of person to compare people to one another, and makes her opinions very clear.
She often refers to my brother Isaac (18M) as "the favourite one" and openly berates my cousin Ellie (17F) for being "flighty" and not knowing what career she wants to go into- and then mocking any career ideas that she does have by saying she wouldn't be any good at them anyway!
Conversely, she lavishes Isaac and male cousin Charlie (19M) with praise for their degree choices, as they both do STEM subjects that are more likely to lead to well paid jobs in the medical or finance sectors.
I feel that this is a symptom of her own values and obsession with hoarding money; whilst not explicitly materialistic, she is very cheap and will never spend money on anything unless it is absolutely necessary.
She mercilessly mocks the "younger generation's reliance" on modern technology such as social media and smartphones, and tells her grandchildren that they are "sad" and "pathetic" for using these things and that her generation were more mature than current 20-somethings.
However, the real kicker is that she actually got a facebook account about six months ago, after criticising it so harshly for so many years...
She uses the account every day to check the profiles of her children and grandchildren.
Grandmother is also the sort of person that enjoys making the same comments over and over, hoping that if she goads you for long enough you will take the bait and snap at her.
Grandmother said, without any hint of irony, that I will "have to wake up early to cook your brother his breakfast" and do Isaac's laundry whilst they are away.
Grandmother is very disapproving of the fact that I drink alcohol at all, saying that it is "unladylike" and that I "should know better".
Obviously, I absolutely take issue with this- it wasn't me that even made the comment, my grandmother had absolutely no knowledge of the context behind it, and I feel like she is clutching at straws in order to find anything to criticise me for.
This is a very unsubtle way for her to tell me that she disapproves of the fact that I have black and Asian friends.
She has always been racist in the sense that she sees non-white people as inferior and therefore their achievements are more impressive, eg "he's a good doctor, even if he is black" or "you can say what you like about Asians but they work very hard!"
I feel like I just cannot win, and like she is looking for things to criticise.
Ugh.
I just feel growing resentment at the way that my grandmother criticises me and other family members all the time, and I am angry at the way that she loves to wind people up and taunt them until they snap.
On the other hand, I feel like she has never done anything awful to any of us, and the way that she says a lot of these mean comments suggest that she thinks it is all a joke and we should all be laughing along with her- sometimes I think I am being oversensitive, but her behaviour and her comments absolutely are hurtful.
