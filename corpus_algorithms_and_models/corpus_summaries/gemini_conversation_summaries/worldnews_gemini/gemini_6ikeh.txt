In 70AD the Romans destroyed the Second Temple and ended Jewish self-rule in Israel. They then renamed the area "Filistina" after the Philistine people who lived to the north of Israel, and who had been the enemies of the Jews.
So after World War 2, the U.N. decided to give East of the Jordan River to the Hashemite Arab kings (kingdom of Jordan), then split the remaining region between Jews and Arabs. The Jews accepted this partition plan, and the Arabs rejected it, in 1948. Thus were born modern Israel and modern Palestine.
