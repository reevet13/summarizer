Although I have family in the Netherlands (near Delft), I can't speak for the Dutch as a whole, let alone those from Amsterdam, but my understanding is this...

What would it be like to be from one of the most progressive cities (and countries) in the world?
Where prostitutes are regulated, where soft drugs are tolerated, where doctors are sometimes permitted (that is, not prosecuted) to perform euthanasia?

And what would it be like to have your city viewed as a den of sin, rather than a haven of tolerance?
How would it feel to have thousands of tourists visit NOT to experience the architecture or the history, but to screw hookers?
How about having your culture mocked because of its toleration?
Or hundreds of boorish twenty-something frat boys making a mess of themselves on the streets during the Cannabis Cup?

The Dutch have taken a very pragmatic view towards drug use, prostitution, and many other societal issues, but that does not mean that they like them.
Rather, they understand that even when these things are outlawed, they still occur anyway.
Many Dutch remain very strictly religious, in accordance with their history as one of the hotbeds of Calvinism (and other forms of Protestantism).

So, the view of the Dutch government for much of the last four hundred years has been to regulate and control vices.
But this has seesawed back and forth many times.
Cannabis use is still technically illegal, existing in a shadowy realm of uncertainty.
See also the above-mentioned euthanasia controversy.
Their government (like any government) is always moving back and forth, left and right.

But let's also keep our wits about us.
Yes, the law that prevents one from standing and drinking at the same time is foolish.
Yes, not being able to sit in a cafe outside after midnight is overbearing.
Yes, some members of Tweede Kamer (among other legislative bodies) want to repeal some of the more liberal policies of their country.

Remember, however, that this is a country that just voted to ban electronic ballots.
You can still engage in semi-legal debauchery without ever having to learn the language.
Hell, the Dutch Police will probably tell you to knock it off, rather than arrest you (I am ashamed, but relieved, to admit that this happened to me).
They know what they're doing.
Let's work on making the States a bit more tolerant like the Dutch before criticizing the way their government handles itself.
