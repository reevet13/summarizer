Having toured the mass graves in France of WWI soldiers, both English and German I can look down upon this in complete contempt.

The most ugly part of this is that whoever did this must have no idea about history.
In at least one English war cemetery, 2 Germans soldiers lie with the English ones.
Why?
Because years afterwards we found their corpses nearby and thought it only fair they get a proper burial even though they were 'the enemy'.
All men are born equal and in death, all men become equal.

There were Jewish graves too, right next to the Christian ones.
They had stones on them rather than crosses etched on the front (some Jewish right I am unaware of, would anybody please enlighten me?) but they all stood together.
Several rows had 3-10 headstones set up so close they were touching.
I asked why.
I was informed those rows contained the mixed body parts of several different victims that they could not separate into separate 'people' so they had to be buried together.
At least 30% of the graves were set up in this manner.
The Menin gate was the worst...thousands upon thousands of names of people they simply could not find but they knew must be dead.
There are thousands of corpses unfound, laying rotting in those fields.
The British war graves weren't the manky unwashed grey you see in normal cemetaries - they were bright, dazzling white.
Cleaned weekly.
The German ones were cheaper and made of grey materials...this was partially due to the fact the Belgians weren't so keen on helping the Germans bury their dead after the war or maintaining their graveyards.

Every year unexploded shells kill the odd farmer in what is known as the 'Iron Harvest'.
They expect that we will keep finding shells this way for up to 800 years.
Local farmers who do not die and find duds sell them to tourists.
I have an old one on my shelf.
I can read the numbers and see the timing dials, but I do not know to this day if it is German or English in origin.
Fitting no?

We should make people tour these graves as a mandatory part of their schooling, not make them swear loyalty oaths.

Whoever did this is ignorant and foolish.
They need to be slapped around with heavy, heavy history books repeatedly.
