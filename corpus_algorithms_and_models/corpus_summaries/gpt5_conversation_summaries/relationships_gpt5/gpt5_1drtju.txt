We dated for almost two years and lived together for about the last 8 months.
In the last two months of the relationship I felt her start to pull away.
We talked about what was wrong and she said repeatedly that she didn't know, but she felt like she might need to be single.
She felt too dependent on me for her happiness, and didn't know how to be on her own or happy with herself.
So we broke up.
A month and a half later we ran into eachother at a concert.
I put her in cab at the end of the night and she texted me when she got home that it was the most fun she's had in a long time.
While at the park we talked about how we each were enjoying time alone.
She realized how important it is for her to feel like she has a life outside of the relationship.
But that it was hard because it feels so easy to end up back where we were, and that it was confusing and scary.
She still feels like she has a lot to figure out about herself.
We agreed to make an effort to continue doing our own things and working on ourselves while we think about what we want and where the other falls into that.
We have since begun hanging out again but we both have doubts about us being ready.
