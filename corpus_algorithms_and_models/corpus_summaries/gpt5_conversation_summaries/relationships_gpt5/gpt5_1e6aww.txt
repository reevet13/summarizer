First of all, thought I'd get this out of the way: I never thought I'd be 'that guy' that dates a girl much younger than him, but until you're put in such a position where you meet someone you consider really special, you don’t have much of an understanding.
Some back story: I first met this girl roughly 200km from my home town, where she was working in a cafe as a waitress.
Upon seeing her facebook, alarm bells were instantly set off as to warning me about her age.
In order to meet up with me, she told her family that she was to stay the night at her friends place, but instead she caught a bus and visited me.
She went out for a bit, came back, and said everything was ok, we start saying our goodbyes as I leave the house.
I look up and at the top of a hill I spot her dad staring at us.
Her dad takes one good look at me in disgust and says “so you’re the guy from the city? You’re not welcome to come here or see my daughter again, now get the fuck out”.
Her dad says that after she turns 18 and leaves for college she is free to do what she wants but until then she is forbidden to see me again.
A lot can happen in a years time, I’m not sure what to think anymore.
