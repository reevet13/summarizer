We've known each other for three years now, extremely close since the beginning.
We dated for a few months at the beginning, and on more than one occasion we have both referred to these months as *the happiest of our lives*.
Eventually, we started talking again, and became closer than ever.
After two years of battling myself, I finally manned up and ended my facade.
We began to write letters and in one of them I told him of how much I missed him and thought of him, in a more than platonic way.
In my next letter I came right out and told him of my own feelings, the fact that I'd been carrying this love for him for so long, that I just wanted to know what to do.
It was not til weeks later that he told me he'd gotten my letter, poured over it millions of times, memorized it.
He told me that for as long as he could remember, he'd been gay.
He told me I was the only girl ever to convince him that perhaps he'd been wrong, but no, as soon as things started to get physical, he knew we could not be.
I want to be his true friend, be there for him for the hell he must be going through, yet I cannot help but cry at my misfortune.
I want to show him I support him and that we can continue being friends, but what can I do?
