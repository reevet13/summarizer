Now, with the exception of a very small percentage of truly lucky individuals, we've all been there: We've all had our hearts torn out, crushed, stepped on, dropped kicked, shredded, and otherwise broken.
And after all my experience with women and dating, I think I've come up with a fool proof way to get over your heartache: **TIME**
See, you have to realize the one simple truth of the matter: You didn't fall IN love with them overnight (not truly), so don't expect to fall OUT of love with them overnight.
The only thing that can heal a broken heart is time.
Let's look at it medically: If you were in a car wreck and shattered your knee, you'd have surgery to **REPAIR** the damage, be in a cast for a few months to **PROTECT** the repairs, and then you'd need to exercise the tendons to **STRENGTHEN** the repairs.
Well, the first and most important step is to REMOVE anything that impairs the healing process.
All those photos, cards, letters, texts, emails, videos, Facebook comments; those are like shrapnel in a broken heart.
And as long as you keep them visible and accessible in your life, you're never going to heal.
Well, I'm not going to argue the "right and wrong" ways, but IMO, the healthiest way is to fill that hole with love... from yourself.
After all, if you don't like/love the person you are, how do yo expect anyone else to?
