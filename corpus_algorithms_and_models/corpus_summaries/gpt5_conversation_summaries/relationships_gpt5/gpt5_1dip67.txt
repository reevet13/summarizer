Years went by and while I was in college, my father passed away unexpectedly.
My mother was in dire straits with her money.
Surprised and sad, I stopped college and took a job in her town.
I moved in with her (with her permission) and spent my next two years in horror learning just how deep the rabbit hole went.
And the worse yet was that the rabbit was none other than the middle sibling, someone 11+ years older than me.
Also, a person she would not allow me to go after legally.
Although she wanted me to fix things, particularly the money problems; she only wanted me to fix them in so much as they would not affect my sibling.
Initially, I'd say I lost a little over a year's pay (and all of my savings from working during college) to digging her out of the hole.
My mother loaned money she didn't have (including mine) at the slick bunny's insistence.
With this financial slight of hand, all of my mother's income for a month was eaten and gone into the ether as though it never existed.
I sit here oddly calm despite having been called and threatened with being beaten at my own father's grave and even killed.
I don't really want a guardianship but as her next of kin and the only one within a thousand miles, l feel obligated to her.
I'm tired of it and also tired of being the adult.
