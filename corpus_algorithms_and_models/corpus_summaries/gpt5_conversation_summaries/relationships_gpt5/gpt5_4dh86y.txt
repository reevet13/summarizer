I have been dating my boyfriend for a little more than a year.
We are talking multiple packs a day.
He told me that he did plan on quitting and that he would cut back.
It SEEMS that he went from 2-3 packs a day to 1 ½ - 2 packs a day by September and then stalled.
In November I got him a quit smoking kit, with candies and snacks that could redirect his oral fixation along with a card of encouragement.
I asked him to consider a quitting goal date, he never came up with one.
So in mid-February I told him since he hadn’t come up with a quit date, I was.
I gave him until April 2nd, and if things didn’t change I was leaving.
The month and a half leading up to the date I didn’t see a change in behavior.
This time I saw cigarette ash in the sink that wasn’t there previously and smelled it.
In the end he offered another explanation, that the humidity of the shower caused the smell to come back and that’s what I was smelling.
My gut tells me that he is lying to me and is willing to go as far as make me think that I’m imagining things (until he came up with his brilliant residual smoke/humidity idea!).
TL;DR- I gave my boyfriend an ultimatum to quit smoking, he ‘has’ and now might be lying and doing it behind my back.
