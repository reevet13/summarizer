Last night, he told me all the things that he's been upset about with me.
The 4 main things he mentioned was how I treat my family, that I wasn't a good source of artistic/general motivation, that we were too different in likes and dislikes to be compatible, and how easily my opinions change.
I managed to talk him into taking a "break" instead of just dropping me outright, but I haven't heard of this working well much.
But in the end, its my fault I yell, and it's something I can change.
He says that I lack motivation, which is true.
As we've dated, we've come to realize we have a few major differences, but we still like a majority of the same things.
This cycle has happened several times on several topics, and it seems like something we can fix instantly with a bit more understanding between the both of us.
This relationship is the best thing to ever happen to me, and the most important thing in my life right now.
