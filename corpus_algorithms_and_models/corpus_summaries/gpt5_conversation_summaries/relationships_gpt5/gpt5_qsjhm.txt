My boyfriend and I moved across the country and moved in together three years ago.
He was becoming more and more depressed, and he was relying on me to keep him happy.
We were so good to each other, but we were living separate lives... and it was causing friction and resentment.
So last year in March I broke-up with him and moved out.
Come August, we decide to get back together, which we both later admitted we knew we would when we both had space to find our autonomy.
**But also while I'm away, he took this opportunity to have sex with two women**, a total of three times, in the bed we bought together.
He'd freak-out, he'd have huge temper tantrums in my apartment, he'd rage in the corner, he'd scream, he'd break things.
I learned, pretty quickly, that I wasn't allowed to express myself or ask questions without this type of over-the-top, exaggerated behavior resulting.
But now, 9 months later, it's clear this is his new coping mechanism.
He disappears for two days, sometimes three, doesn't call, email, text, anything.
He makes me dinner, we are back to normal.
Rinse, wash, repeat.
I'm afraid that I'm already walking on egg-shells.
And I'm still too hurt about him cheating and all the years of sexual rejection to put up with a new form of abuse.
