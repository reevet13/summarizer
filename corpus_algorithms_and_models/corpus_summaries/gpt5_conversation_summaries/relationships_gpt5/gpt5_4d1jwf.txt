Mike did, however, tell me that he was tired of me having to pay for everything for us, so before joining me in this new city he wanted to move back in with his parents and start applying for full time jobs in new city and be long distance until he found work, then could move over with me.
Mike tells me he would be happy to move right away, but he doesn't want a "shitty" part time job and will only come over to live with me if I pay for him to move, then continue paying 100% for both of us to live here until he finds a full time job that he enjoys.
My problem with this is in the years since we've graduated he's been very picky about what jobs he likes, in fact I've never once heard him say he liked the idea of any job except for being a writer so I don't know how long it would take for him to get this perfect full time job he wants.
