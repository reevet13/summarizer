Late last year, a childhood friend of mine (whom I don't often speak to, but definitely see time to time) started to date this girl who I somewhat knew of, but did not actually know her.
We will call them Ron and Grace.
Because of mutual friends, we continued to see each other, and even traveled with some friends and Ron and Grace.
Ron leaves on a out of the country trip, and long-story-short, he comes back and breaks it off with Grace.
Then that girl tells me that Grace told her that she would marry me.
In sort of an embarassed/laughing manner, Grace looks at me and says "heh, yeah I did say that."
The following week I spent everyday with Grace.
At a friend's bday party, Grace tells me how her and her friend decided that they were going to quit their jobs and move to another country for a year.
Being the free spirit she is, she already had her ticket.
I can't recall exactly when, but at sometime during those weeks, I started to like her.
Seeing her made me happy, and not seeing her for too long made me miss her so much.
I'm starting to feel like I can't live without her, let alone go a year without seeing her.
A long distance would not work, and it would be hard on her also.
She leaves in a few months.
I haven't felt this way since Tyra, and that was almost 3 years ago.
