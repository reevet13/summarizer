In February 2015, my girlfriend of just a little over a year and I broke up over a terrible mistake I made.
However, instead of talking about my insecurities, I would flirt with other women in hopes of them giving me an ego boost.
The guilt from the incident led me to attempt suicide.
From that day on, we would still hang out and do everything we usually did and tell each other that we love each other.
Eventually, in November we would have a sit down conversation where we both agreed we needed to actually breakup.
From then on we fell back into our old pattern, minus saying we loved the other.
When I returned to town on a Saturday, I had realized I was blocked on all social media by her.
But when venting to my friend about it all, he revealed to me that apparently she had made out with that guy two months ago and was telling people she thought she had a crush on him.
So I had attempted to commit suicide after telling her where she could find my body.
We briefly spoke on what was happening, and she told me that while she had a crush on him, the feelings weren't deep until "about a week ago" which was only a day after we had sex.
I returned home and was admitted to a mental hospital for a week to help rid myself of the suicidal thoughts that have plagued my mind.
