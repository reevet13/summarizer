December 2010, Senior year I start dating a girl, let's call her Mary.
We hit it off and become inseparable.
Everything's going good until June of 2011.
I listen and leave her.
Fast forward two months of feeling terrible, I contact her and ask her to meet.
I said how stupid I was and begged her to let me back into her life.
Hesitantly, she does.
I go to a university about an hour and a half away from our town.
She stays and goes to community college.
It was amazing.
I'm given an enormous workload I was completely unprepared for.
Needless to say, I freaked out and lashed out at the only thing I had control over, Mary.
I broke up with her and gave her the excuse I wasn't happy.
So i try dating this girl on my floor.
It lasts about two weeks and the whole time, I'm thinking of Mary.
This is real life and I only have one chance to get what I really want in this short life.
We text and she tells me how much she **hates** me for what I did to her.
She gets infuriated at how I could do that to her, given I love her so much.
We talk on the phone and she is pretty much ripping me apart.

But the thought of what I did still holds her back.
