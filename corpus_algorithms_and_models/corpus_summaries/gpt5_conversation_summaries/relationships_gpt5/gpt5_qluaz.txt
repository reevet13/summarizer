We unfortunately decided to take all the same classes last year, which led to a break-up in November.
Thankfully, we reunited, worked things out, and things were pretty awesome for the past few months...until the last few weeks transpired.

In fact, H told me both during and days after the trip that he intended to stay with me "for the long-haul".

H struggled with this harder than I did, and I'm not entirely sure he ever recovered.
Fights started popping up again, for no reason.

H retaliated angrily and told me not to tell him what "he should talk to his therapist about".
H told me he was "extremely unhappy with the way the relationship has been" and that although he "loves me and wants to be with me" that he doesn't know if he can be in a relationship right now.
