I was officially diagnosed with OCD about a year ago, but I have had the same compulsions for about three or four years.
The symptoms themselves revolve around food.
I have a crippling revulsion to solid fat and a small range of other textures.
If I consume it or even think I consumed it, I'll get severely nauseated, no longer be able to eat, and sometimes throw up if the exposure is too large.
Then, for up to four days, every time I eat I'll have intrusive thoughts about the exposure which brings the nausea back and sometimes keeps me from eating unless I'm eating extremely 'safe' foods.
Red meat is the biggest problem food, but I know the restaurants in my area that are good about serving lean meat and can still eat well cleaned red meat.
Despite this, since my compulsions have started, I have constantly butted heads with people over my OCD.
The first major problem is that some of my friends have watched way too much of 'my strange addiction' and think that if I just face my fears and just eat it, I'll be cured.
When I try to explain that I would probably have major health problems if I try that, they dismiss me as not really wanting help.
These people like to bring me to my problem restaurants or cook me my problem foods 'for my own good.'
So I don't know where to go from here anymore.
