For the most part, I would say our marriage is a happy one.
I have noticed, however, a pattern that has emerged over the years, and I'm concerned both about the fact that it seems to keep happening and also that the damage it does seems to accumulate over time.
The most recent blowup happened this morning.
He shoves me, firmly, away, and declares that he's "already taken care of it" and I can tell that he's fuming.
It was forceful, unexpected, and as such, scared me.
In fact, he asserts he didn't even touch me.
He just begins screaming at me, hurling insults (calling me a bitch, telling me I'm fat and lazy, etc.).
Our poor, sweet, son just does NOT know what to make of his daddy screaming at me like that.
Aside from the yelling in front of our son, it is this series of behaviors that is the pattern.
Something will trigger a blowup, and next thing I know, I'm hearing months worth of pent-up frustration that I didn't know existed hurled at me all at once, with all of the venom and fury to go with it.
But, inevitably, time passes and then another blowup happens out of the blue again.
And each time, I just feel like it's chipping away at my soul a little bit more.
Mind you, these are all insults meant for the jugular.
Thoughts about food, calories, exercise, and my body image are ever-present struggles.
