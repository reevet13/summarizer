My wife and I have been together for about 5 years now.
We got married two years ago.
Things seemed really great when we met and for the first few years I was incredibly happy and in love.
It only seemed to get bad around the time we got married.
After a long string of denials, I found out she had a romantic interest in this guy.
What followed was the most emotionally painful period of time I have ever endured.
While she was moved out, she started seeing two additional guys.
I felt at the time like she was on so much stuff that she just wasn't herself.
She wrecked her car twice during this period.
I make a decision that I am done with her and ask her for a divorce, in the same week that she finds out she's going to have to go to Colorado for an internship.
Things seem much more stable; she still probably smokes too much weed, but she's off all the other crap, and I don't believe she's still cheating.
The thing is, it seems like something within me has changed.
I don't feel like I can trust her.
But since we're married, and especially because this is my second marriage, it seems like a much harder decision.
I desperately want it to work, but I'm not sure I will ever feel the same way about her again, not sure I will ever be able to trust her.
