I live in Egypt, and living conditions here are getting worse by the day.
There were recent killings at some areas over who gets to buy the bread first.
All necessary food items have risen in price by more than 50% (sometimes 100%) in the past year and people's salaries remain the same.
The poor are actually fighting (and recently, dying) for bread and basic life necessities.
We have a general strike on the 6th of April.
The strike is against the failure of the government to do anything about the ridiculous rise in prices of basic life necessities.
Egypt is currently experiencing the largest and most sustained strike wave since WWII.
Problem is that Egypt imports nearly most of its wheat (in fact Egypt is a net food importer), that's why it's so vulnerable to international surges in price.
Egypt used to be a net food exporter but was in part forced by the World Bank to switch to growing mainly cotton for export (export led raw material dogma of development technocrats).
