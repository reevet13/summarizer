Russia in 2008 is nothing short of an economic miracle.
The average Russian wage earner has seen his wages increase 10 fold in the last 8 years of Putin rule.
The currency and government are both stable.
Foreign debt has been repaid and there is a hundred billion dollar surplus which is being used to rebuild the long neglected Soviet infrastructure.
Most Russians have little if any debt, own their own residence outright, and have considerable savings which they are spending on a now booming retail/commercial market.
If America makes the same relative gains to it's economy post-crash that Russia has made post-communism, then the long term future of America looks very bright indeed.
There is no doubt that Russians were much more disaster-ready than Americans are.
However, I think that American economy is MUCH more stable than Soviet Union's was in 1985-1991.
Life, governments, and economies are cyclical.
The US media is far from perfect, but it is also far from the kind of government control seen in Russia.
This article is a fun read, but it really seems out of touch with reality to assume that the US would collapse in the same way the USSR did.
Nobody is ever prepared for a collapse.
It is however about avoiding panic.
