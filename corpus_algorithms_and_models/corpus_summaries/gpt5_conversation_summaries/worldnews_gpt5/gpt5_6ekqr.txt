Riots over food rock Yemen.
>Rising food prices helped trigger the protests.
The price of wheat has doubled since  February, while rice and vegetable oil have gone up 20%.
Food prices are sky rocketing all over the arab world.
Arab governments, except UAE , are doing nothing to counter it.
At the behest of the WTO, nations began opening themselves up to more rice importation at the cost of their own rice production.
Thus we see global rice prices having doubled in the 12 month time frame since last year.
Rising incentives for biofeul crops have in areas capped the amount of land used for rice or as seen in others, began reversing the amount of available land.
Dropping currency value is NOT the main cause of rising food prices.
The demand for ethanol is.
A few years ago 5% of the U.S. corn supply went to ethanol.
This year 20% did.
Since wheat and soybeans compete with corn for land, their price has gone up as well.
> There have long been tax breaks to encourage production of ethanol, and last year President Bush signed a law mandating a substantial increase in the use of renewable fuels, principally ethanol, over the next 15 years.
>Bolivia, Honduras, and Pakistan have banned wheat or corn exports, while China has imposed or increased export taxes on wheat, corn, barley, oats and soybeans, in order to deter exports of those commodities.
