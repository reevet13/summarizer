De esta forma, y por primera vez en 30 años, Argentina abandonó su adhesión a la postura estadounidense de perseguir tanto al traficante de drogas como al consumidor.
Drug dealing is still illegal, the new policy is: "don't target the consumer, target the dealer"
The point being that if you punish the consumer, the dealers will just find more consumers to sell.
If you target the dealers, the consumers won't have anyone to buy drugs to, and will stop consuming altogether.
Buying is not illegal, but rather there is no longer a punishment: decriminalized.
You don't know this because The New York Times and it's satellite propaganda outlets won't report it to you, but drug use is decriminalized in a great many places around the world.
Portugal, Italy, Russia lead the way, with many other countries in varying states of decriminalization, like the Netherlands.
