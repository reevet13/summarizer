Can she run as an independent should she not get the nomination?  (I am asking this seriously)
Technically, yes.  Practically, no.  She would need to get on the ballot in all states.  If she just wants to spoil it, she could do it in a few states.  It would probably help Obama if she did.  The people who would vote for her, but not Obama, won't be voting for McCain.
If her strategy really is the Tonya Harding, "elect McCain and I'll try again in 2012" idea, this would be the way to do it. She couldn't win, but neither could Obama.
It is too early to say this with any confidence, IMHO, but I seriously have to ask who the hell thought this strategy would have any positive consequences given the general mood of this country - a mood which can be described as fear and loathing.  About the economy, about the war, about the very future of US civilization.
If ever there was a time people in the US wanted someone to light a candle rather than curse the darkness, it is now.
I think we can all agree that Obama is the most likely winner. And unless something changes should be the winner. However, I do like the idea personally of letting all of the states vote and have an actual say.
She doesn't have to beat him.  She just has to take away his margin.  That would likely only be a few percent.
